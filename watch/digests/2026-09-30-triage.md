- **HMH**: resale S-1 (filed 28 Sep). [S-1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2021880/000119312526405527/d32439ds1.htm)
  The Selling Stockholders (Principal Stockholders: Baker Hughes and Akastor affiliates) register up to **31,891,652** Class A shares. There are only **12,201,501** Class A shares outstanding (as of 28 Sep), so the registered supply is about 2.6× the current Class A float. The filing says the 180-day IPO lock-up **expired 27 Sep 2026**, and the Class B / B.V. holders can now redeem into Class A (up to 12 times a year).
  **Strategy:** overhang. **Why it matters:** this is already the first case in `strategies/overhang/RULES.md`. The filing confirms how big the supply is compared with the float. The next trigger is the **EFFECT** notice, after which the stock can be sold into the market. Watch for it, then for the absorption signals.

- **BRSP** (BrightSpire Capital): Schedule 13D. [filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1717547/000092189526002666/0000921895-26-002666-index.htm)
  CWRE Special Situations (CW Investment Advisers) owns **8.7%** (10,982,257 shares) and has switched from a 13G to a 13D. The board refused its requests to waive the 9.8% REIT ownership limit twice (12 Aug and 14 Sep). CWRE says the shares trade at about 57¢ per dollar of undepreciated book value and that it "can no longer remain passive".
  **Strategy:** activist 13D. **Why it matters:** an active holder is now publicly fighting a board over a stock trading near its lows. Watch for a proxy fight or letters to shareholders.

- **MNY** (MoneyHero): Schedule 13D. [filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1974044/000092189526002662/0000921895-26-002662-index.htm)
  The Honig group owns **9.0%** in total. On 29 Sep Jonathan Honig sent a public letter asking the board to "explore a sale" now. He cites uncertainty over leadership, stalled growth and insiders' lack of confidence.
  **Strategy:** activist 13D. **Why it matters:** the letter publicly asks for a sale. Caution: this is a small foreign issuer, and the filer's track record is worth checking before relying on it.

- **PPLI** (People Inc): Schedule 13D. [filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1800227/000090514826004287/0000905148-26-004287-index.htm)
  HighSage Ventures owns **6.6%** (Jennifer Stier is deemed to own 5,394,779 shares). On 29 Sep it sent a letter to Barry Diller and MGM's chair supporting a possible MGM bid for People Inc. That bid follows People Inc withdrawing its own proposal to buy MGM.
  **Strategy:** activist 13D, but it's about takeover speculation rather than a forced seller, so it's a low fit. **Why it matters:** a 6.6% holder is pushing for a bid. Treat it as context only.
