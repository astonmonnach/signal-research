**Why it was flagged.** Logged on 4 Oct at **$1.16** (also by the 28 Sep market scan at $1.26), on two counts:

**Status.**
- The **annual meeting on 14 Oct** votes on a **1:3–1:20 reverse split** and on approval to issue more than 20% below the Nasdaq minimum price ([DEF 14A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1622345/000149315226042901/formdef14a.htm)). The prior split was 1:7 in Nov 2024.
- **Cash was $183K at 30 Jun**, and the company has going-concern doubt.
- 5 Oct ~$1.14.

**Risk.** Variable-price conversions mean every leg down creates more shares. This is the clearest dilution machine in the group.
