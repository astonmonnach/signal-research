## 3. Evidence
**The award and the order (primary):**
- $995m is a ceiling: "not a commitment to purchase that amount" (29 Sep release). **The $125m order is real money.** It is 12.6% of the ceiling and ~16% of the ~$780m market cap at the award. Spread over 24 months it is ~$62m/yr, or 5% of 2025 sales (~$1.16bn), but **~77% of 2025's entire A&D sales ($161.4m)**.
- Solicitation SP800026R0016 was posted 2 Jun 2026, so the requirement was public four months early. *(Lead sources, not primary: [HigherGov](https://www.highergov.com/news/metallus-wins-up-to-995-million-dla-contract-for-6490052/), [solicitation copy](https://govcontractfinder.com/contracts/solicitation-for-hf1-steel-idiq-sp800026r0016).)*
- Volume: maximum **438.48M lb over 5 years (~219k short tons)**, year 1 up to **104.44M lb (~52k tons)**. That is about equal to **all of Metallus's 2025 A&D shipments (55.9k tons)**, and 8% of 2025 total tons (631.7k). $995m divided by the maximum tons is ~$4,540/ton, against a realized A&D price of $3,005/ton in Q2 2026, so the dollar ceiling has headroom.
- **Not a renewal.** USAspending shows no federal prime contracts for Metallus or TimkenSteel since FY2008. The only record is a 2022 Air Force EWAAC on-ramp with $0 obligated. HF-1 used to go through the commercial shell-forging chain, so this is Metallus's first meaningful federal prime contract.

**Defence is already material** ([10-K FY2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1598428/000119312526061042/mtus-20251231.htm), [10-Q Q2 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1598428/000119312526332244/mtus-20260630.htm)):
- A&D sales were $115.0m (2023, 8% of sales), $134.9m (2024, 12%) and $161.4m (2025, 14%).
- H1 2026 A&D was **$112.0m vs $74.6m (+50%)**, or 17% of H1 sales. Q2 2026 alone was $60.1m on 20.0k tons.
- H1 2026 sales by end market (10-Q revenue table): industrial $228.4m (35%), automotive $240.8m (37%), A&D $112.0m (17%), energy $57.0m (9%), other $11.1m; total $649.3m. A&D earns the highest price per ton ($2,971 vs a $1,921 average).
- Metallus has made HF-1 continuously since 1993 ([army.mil, 2 Sep 2026](https://www.army.mil/article/295062/the_u_s_army_and_metallus_kickoff_hf_1_steel_capacity_expansion_effort_with_new_manufacturing_assets_in_ohio)).

## 7. Risks and what kills it
- **Backlog ≠ revenue** (METHOD failure mode 5). The $125m is deliverable over up to 24 months, so revenue may not show until 2027.
- **The stockpile pot is oversubscribed** (≈$4.0bn of ceilings against $2bn). A second order is not assured.
- **Steel is cyclical.** Automotive and industrial were 72% of H1 2026 sales, so they matter more than defence. H1 operating cash flow was negative.
- **CEO transition on 1 Jan 2027**, plus ≈150k more officer 10b5-1 shares.
- **Kill triggers:** a close below $17.00; Q3 with no backlog or A&D uplift; DLA cancelling or downsizing the order.
