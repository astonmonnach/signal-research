## 1. What it is, and why it's on the watchlist
- **Rayonier Advanced Materials** makes high-purity cellulose (cellulose specialties for acetate and ethers, plus commodity viscose and fluff) and runs a paperboard / high-yield-pulp (HYP) segment.
- Q2 2026 sales $376M, adjusted EBITDA $40M; **LTM adjusted EBITDA ≈ $136M** ([Q2 release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1597672/000159767226000027/exhibit991ryamq22026.htm)).
- Adjusted net debt $755M (**5.6×**). Covenant net secured leverage was 4.2× against a **4.75× maximum, stepping down to 4.50× from 2027** ([10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1597672/000159767226000029/ryam-20260627.htm); [10-K debt note](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1597672/000159767226000010/ryam-20251231.htm)).
- 67.6M shares, so a ~$480M market cap; EV ≈ $1.24bn, ~9× LTM EBITDA.
- **Found:** first catalyst scan (X post), 28 Sep, at **$8.15**, with the note "potential sale; expected +$1–3". 2 Oct close $7.12 (−12.6%, the worst idea in the ledger so far); 5 Oct ~$7.10.

## 3. Evidence
**For:**
- Cellulose-specialties prices **+21% y/y**, +8% q/q; Q2 came in "ahead of expectations"; the company still targets positive 2026 free cash flow.
- **Trade protection:**
  - A Section 301 tariff on Brazilian dissolving pulp took effect 22 Jul (Federal Register 2026-14542; the 37.5% rate is per RYAM's 10-Q).
  - **Preliminary duties:** anti-dumping 7.20% on Brazil and 6.54% on Norway; countervailing 3.67% on Brazil (FR 2026-10523, 2026-10527, 2026-05805).
- Executives received leveraged performance units on 27 Aug that pay only if the stock rises ≥25% (Forms 4).

**Against:**
- Cellulose-specialties **volumes −23% y/y**.
- Paperboard/HYP adjusted EBITDA −$10M, plus a $13M HYP impairment.
- Leverage of 5.6× with ~13% covenant headroom.
- The board has **already said no to $11–12 once**.
- The former chair sold 1,931 shares at $9.13 (7 Aug).
- Dimensional cut its holding to 4.9%.

## 5. Branches
- **MERC (Mercer), the scare that isn't the same story:**
  - On 1 Oct Mercer "elected not to make the approximately $25.8 million interest payment due October 1, 2026 on the 2028 Notes". It is in a 30-day grace period (to ~31 Oct), in "advanced discussions" with a group holding over 75% of its notes on "a comprehensive recapitalization… and a significant capital infusion" ([8-K, 2 Oct](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1333274/000119312526411343/d108140d8k.htm)).
  - Mercer already had going-concern doubt in its [Q2 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1333274/000119312526338272/merc-20260630.htm).
  - **This is commodity NBSK/NBHK pulp plus German costs plus 2027 maturities. RYAM has no cellulose-specialties overlap with it, no maturities before 2028–29, and no going-concern language.**
  - The real read-across is lender and buyer appetite for leveraged pulp assets, which could weigh on bids **[INFERENCE]**. MERC equity at ~$0.25 is likely to be heavily diluted.
- **Competitors under the trade cases:** Bracell (private), Borregaard (BRG.OL), Suzano (SUZ).
- **Customer:** Eastman (cellulose acetate, Kingsport) imports high-purity dissolving pulp (FR 2026-02355 FTZ notice); likely a RYAM customer **[INFERENCE]**.
- **Bidder side:** AIP/GCF; International Paper (GCF's former parent).
- **Commodity:** the BLS wood-pulp PPI (WPU0911) was 218.4 (preliminary) in Aug 2026, +9.5% since February and −3.5% y/y. That is commodity pulp, not cellulose specialties.
- **Forced flows:** RYAM is in the Russell 2000 (iShares IWM 1.64M shares) and Micro-Cap (0.21M) per June N-PORT filings. No change event.

- A negative ITC vote; covenant pressure in 2027 (4.50×); pulp-sector financing stress after MERC.
- **Kill:** a "standalone" announcement, a negative ITC final vote, or a close below ~$5.5 without news.
