## 3. Evidence
**Deal terms** (THRM and MOD 8-Ks, 1 Oct; [MOD 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/67347/000110465926112870/tm2626618d1_8k.htm)):
- 0.44619 THRM per MOD share.
- Cash to Modine of $155,991,275, cut from $210m by the exchange-ratio adjustment.
- A new **$250m senior secured delayed-draw term loan** (SOFR + 1.125–2.000%), fully drawn, with interest-cover and net-leverage covenants.
- PT valued at "approximately $946.4 million" on THRM's 30 Sep close.
- Authorised shares doubled to 110M.

**Special dividend:** "$63,500,492, or $2.07 per share… payable… October 7, 2026, to Gentherm shareholders of record as of… September 28, 2026". New MOD-holder shares don't get it.

**Standalone business** ([Q2 release, 23 Jul](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/903129/000119312526313204/thrm-ex99_1.htm)):
- Record Q2 revenue of $416.2m (+11%). Automotive climate and comfort revenue grew +14.1%, beating light-vehicle production by 14 points.
- **2026 guidance raised** to revenue $1.55–1.65bn and adjusted EBITDA $185–200m. Net leverage ~0.3×.
- $690m of new automotive awards in the quarter.

**Combined, rough [ESTIMATE]:**
- EBITDA: THRM standalone ~$190m plus PT ~$145m (Q1 FY27 annualised) ≈ $335m.
- Net debt ≈ $280m: June net debt of $60m, plus the $156m cash paid to Modine and the $63.5m dividend, funded by the term loan.
- EV ≈ $2.07bn, so **≈6.2× EBITDA**. The pro forma 8-K/A (~17 Dec) will replace this estimate.

- **Integration and dis-synergy costs**, and a messy first combined quarter.
- **Kill triggers:** a close below $28.92; combined guidance below ~$300m EBITDA; no buyback in Q4.
