## 1. What it is, and why it's on the watchlist
- **The Elmet Group** (HQ Portland, Maine; plant in Lewiston) makes **molybdenum and tungsten** wire, rod, powder and parts, plus high-power microwave equipment (EMP). **By shipments it is mainly molybdenum: 59.4% Mo vs 19.3% W in Q2** ([10-Q, qtr to 3 Jul](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2101698/000162828026056460/elmt-20260703.htm), "Shipments by Type"). Calling it a "tungsten pure-play" is wrong.
- Aerospace, Defense & Government was **39.3% of Q2 revenue**. Missile programmes: Javelin, Hellfire, Patriot, PrSM.
- LTM revenue $228.5M, adjusted EBITDA $31.8M ([8-K 2 Oct deck](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2101698/000121390026106420/ea0307420-8k_elmet.htm)).

## 3. Evidence
- **The $150M is "guaranteed funded"** (press release, Ex. 99.1), **but delivery is far off.** ELMT "does not intend to begin delivering material into the National Defense Stockpile until sufficient incremental supply becomes available". No delivery order has been disclosed. The guided ERT gross margin is ~10–15%, so the minimum is worth roughly $15–22M of gross profit, spread over years **[INFERENCE]**.
- **The $2B ceiling is 3.3× the market cap at $19.64. The guaranteed $150M is ~0.25×.** The headline number is the ceiling.
- **The DoW deal terms bind the company.** The DoW gets a board seat and consent rights, and **dividends and buybacks are banned**. A resale shelf for the warrant shares is due within 60 days, i.e. by **~13 Nov**.
- **Feedstock** (424B4): more than 99% non-PRC, from Masan (Vietnam) and EQ Resources (Barruecopardo, Spain). Powder is made in Lewiston and at H.C. Starck Canada. Molybdenum comes from Freeport (Climax) and Molymet.
- **Cash calls absorb most of the $200M [INFERENCE].** The Masan 4.99% stake costs **$124.75M** ([8-K 24 Sep](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2101698/000121390026102840/ea0306067-8k_elmet.htm); settlement was expected 5 Oct). The Springer JV is ~$75M plus ~$75M into Blue Moon. The OSRAM asset purchase is at a negative €18M. Balance sheet at 3 Jul, before the DoW money: cash $66.1M, debt $10.5M.
- **Other dilution:** a Blue Moon warrant for 1,166,970 shares at $21.423 ([8-K 22 Sep](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2101698/000121390026101918/ea0306130-8k_elmet.htm)), and Cantor's IPO warrants (~148k shares at $17.50).
