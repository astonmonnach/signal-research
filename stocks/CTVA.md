## 3. Evidence
**Balance sheet at the spin** (pro forma, 30 Jun, CTVA 8-K Ex. 99):
- Cash $3,058m against borrowings of $1,013m, so **net cash ≈ $2.0bn**.
- Pension/OPEB liabilities $1,215m.
- The seed business sent about $3,078m of cash to EIDP before the spin, plus a further $726–776m (Information Statement, capitalisation note 2).
- EIDP also issued $700m of 6.000% notes due 2036 ([8-K 24 Sep](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1755672/000119312526401156/d170896d8k.htm)).

**Valuation at ~$12.12:**
- Market cap ≈ $8.08bn. EV ≈ $7.25bn including pension, so **EV/2025 EBITDA ≈ 5.7×**, or about 4.7× before pension.
- That is a crop-protection pure play at a distressed-peer multiple.

**What New Corteva says** ([1 Oct release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/corteva-begins-new-chapter-as-focused-innovation-led-global-crop-protection-leader-302895869.html)):
- Crop protection revenue rose "by over $1 billion" from 2020 to 2025, and operating EBITDA margin expanded ~250bp.
- Differentiated products are "~65% of sales". The pipeline is "$11 billion".
- **It gives no standalone guidance, no dividend and no buyback.** Pre-spin Corteva guided FY2026 operating EBITDA of $4.1–4.3bn for both halves combined ([8-K 30 Jul](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1755672/000175567226000022/ctva-20260730.htm)).

**Liabilities:**
- "New Corteva will retain… historical PFAS and other environmental liabilities" (Vylor Information Statement).
- The 18-state fraudulent-transfer suit argues that the seed business was moved away from creditors. If it succeeded, CTVA's creditors would be the beneficiaries ([8-K 30 Sep](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1755672/000119312526409549/d23835d8k.htm)).

**Insiders:** no Form 4 purchases since the spin (EDGAR, checked 5 Oct ~16:00 UK). CFO Jeff Rudolph signed the completion 8-K ([0001193125-26-409895](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1755672/000119312526409895/d116808d8k.htm)).
