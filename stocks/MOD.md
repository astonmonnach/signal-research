## 1. What it is, and why it's on the watchlist
- Modine, soon to be renamed **Modexus Solutions** (ticker stays MOD), is what's left after the Performance Technologies spin-merge into Gentherm. It sells data-centre cooling (the Airedale brand), commercial HVAC and refrigeration.
- Continuing segments in Q1 FY27 (June quarter): **Data Centers $348.6m (+90%)** and Commercial HVAC $261.6m (+22%), so Data Centers is ~57% of continuing sales ([Q1 release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/67347/000110465926088230/mod-20260729xex99d1.htm)). 53.1M shares; ~$9.8bn market cap.

## 3. Evidence
**Q1 FY27 segments** (release above):
- Data Centers: adjusted EBITDA $51.7m, gross margin **20.2%, down 960bp**, hit by "supply chain constraints… production inefficiencies".
- Commercial HVAC: adjusted EBITDA $41.6m.
- Performance Technologies (now gone): $277.8m of sales, $36.2m adjusted EBITDA.
- Total debt $528.2m against $95.3m cash at 30 Jun, so net debt $432.9m. After the $156m prepayment, ≈$277m **[ESTIMATE]**.

**FY27 outlook** (still including PT): sales +20% to +35%, adjusted EBITDA $650–680m. Removing PT's ~$145m/yr leaves ~$505–535m for the continuing business **[INFERENCE]**. EV ≈ $10.1bn, so **≈19–20× continuing EBITDA**. That is a growth multiple.

**Labels:** EDGAR shows SIC 3714 (Motor Vehicle Parts), and Yahoo shows Consumer Cyclical / Auto Parts. No S&P or MSCI reclassification notice has been found.
