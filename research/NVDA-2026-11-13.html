The Huang Stake

If a divorce split Jensen Huang's Nvidia shares and half were sold, the selling would barely register in Nvidia's trading. The thing that moved the stock is already here: the founder has left the company.

Rung 1What happened

FiledOn 11 Nov the board accepted Huang's resignation as President, CEO and director, effective immediately. CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO while the board searches, and long-time director Harvey Jones is now chair [1].

ReportedOn 7 Nov the Financial Times reported a US government memo saying Huang had a relationship with an executive at a Singapore-based Nvidia customer. On 8 Nov Huang told staff he had been unfaithful, and the board opened an independent investigation [9]. The espionage claims are unproven, and China has denied them. The rumour that there is no prenup is unverified.

Rung 2What it actually means

California splits community property equally unless the couple agree something different [6]. Taking the shares held directly, in the living trust and in its LLCs (671.1M) as the shared pot, half is 335.5M shares, about $55.8B, or 1.4% of Nvidia. The 140.5M in the irrevocable trusts probably sit outside a split, because those trusts own them inference.

The selling limit you remembered from ELMT is Rule 144. An insider can sell, in any 3 months, the bigger of 1% of the shares (about 241M for Nvidia) or the average weekly volume over the last four weeks (about 982M lately) [5]. ELMT trades little, so the limit bit hard there. Nvidia trades so much that the limit is bigger than the whole half-stake. While married, a spouse's sales count together with the insider's because they share a home [5]. After a divorce they wouldn't. A non-insider owning under 5% files nothing, so you might never see those sales in filings.

The precedent is Bezos. In 2019 about 19.5M Amazon shares went to MacKenzie Bezos, about a quarter of his stake rather than half. She handed him their votes through "a voting agreement and proxy" [7]. On the day the settlement was announced (4 Apr 2019) Amazon closed down 0.1% while the S&P 500 rose 0.3%, and 20 sessions later it was up 7.1% [8].

Since the story broke, Nvidia has lost about $493B of value, around nine times the whole hypothetical half-stake. Nvidia fell 11.0% from the 6 Nov close to the 12 Nov close while the chip ETF SMH rose 8.1%. That 19-point gap is Nvidia-specific.

Rung 3Who wins and who loses

Production first. Selling shares moves money between investors. None of it reaches Nvidia, so wafers, memory orders and deliveries don't change. Nvidia's chips are made by TSMC and Samsung, with memory from SK Hynix, Micron and Samsung [4]. Production only changes if customers buy less or governments allow less.

  • AMD and Broadcom are where the money went. Both rose while Nvidia fell, which fits buyers moving to the alternatives. AMD's own results came on 3 Nov, before this window.
  • Micron and TSMC sell to everyone making AI chips, so demand holds whoever leads Nvidia.
  • Super Micro lives on Nvidia GPUs, but it also had its own results on 3 Nov, so not all of its fall is about Nvidia.
  • The index funds barely noticed. Nvidia is about a $4.0T company, and its fall was offset by the other chip makers.

Rung 4What could happen next

Base: the business carries on

When CEOs left over relationships before, the stock dipped and then moved on. Intel in June 2018 fell 2.4% on the day (chip ETF −1.2%) and was −2.3% after 20 sessions. McDonald's in Nov 2019 fell 2.7% and was +1.2% after 20 sessions [8]. The difference is that Huang founded Nvidia, which is why the gap this time is about 19 points rather than 2.

Bear: the probe finds a security problem

If it does, the risk is tighter export rules on Asian customers and resellers, which would hit Nvidia's sales, not just its share price. Reports say Congress has asked Huang to testify on 18 Nov reported. The board has given no date for its findings.

The divorce branch (hypothetical)

Bezos shows the likely shape: a negotiated share split, often less than half of the founder's stake, with voting left with the founder and sales spread over years [7]. At Nvidia's volume, even a fast sale would be a few days of normal trading.

Signals to watch

  • 17 Nov: Q3 results and the call under the interim CEO [10]. Watch the guidance and whether customers mention changing orders.
  • Form 144 or Form 4 from Huang: a Form 144 means a planned sale.
  • The board's findings and the CEO search: an outside hire, or Kress kept on.
  • Any 8-K or rule on export licences that names Singapore or Southeast Asian resellers.

For your postThe angle, in your own words

  • The sleeper: the scary scenario, a divorce sale, is small next to what already happened. It's a few days of volume, against about $493B already lost.
  • The teaching point: the Rule 144 limit depends on how much a stock trades. It bit for ELMT and doesn't for Nvidia.
  • Keep the rumours out of it. Post what's filed: the resignation, the share counts and the limits.
