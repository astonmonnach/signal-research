## 1. SKYA: the tender is live; the tax decides who it is for

- **The filings:**
  - [SC TO-I](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1737995/000093041326003076/0000930413-26-003076-index.htm), filed 16 Oct. A [release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1737995/000149315226047520/ex99-1.htm) on 19 Oct announced the commencement and repeated the 29 Oct expiry.
  - $3.00 cash for up to 95% of each class: 48,311,489 shares and 18,775,877 warrants, up to $201.26M.
  - Cash $223.8M, and no digital assets after the SOL sale on 15 Oct.
  - No odd-lot priority. No SOL condition.
  - Conditions are judged by the company, including a competing proposal, a new 5% buyer and its own view of delisting risk.
- **Timing:** Rule 13e-4(f)(1)(i) requires an issuer tender to stay open at least twenty business days. 29 Oct is the tenth from 16 Oct, so an extension to about 12 or 13 Nov is likely (inference; no relief is disclosed).
- **Tax:** non-US holders face 30% withholding on the gross unless they file a W-8.
  - With a W-8BEN at the 15% UK treaty rate (an assumption, not in the filing), $3.00 nets about $2.55.
  - The market price is $2.79 today (+5.5%).
- **Already run:** $1.82 on 9 Oct, then the first trade after the weekend news was $2.335 (12 Oct open). The big move happened in the gap.
- **Verdict:** **tracked, no call.** For this account, selling in the market beats tendering unless the tax can be reclaimed.
