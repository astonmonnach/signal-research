## 1. BIAF: an ATM that can sell about as many shares as outsiders own

- **The supply:**
  - "we are offering up to an additional aggregate of $5,228,226 of our common stock for sale under the Sales Agreement". The company "offered and sold $4,000,000" under the small-company cap already ([424B5, 3 Nov](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1712762/000149315226049806/form424b5.htm)).
  - At the $2.91 price in the filing, that is about 1.8M shares. Shares held outside the company's insiders are "1,813,011", so the ATM covers about 99% of them (arith).
- **More to come:** on 20 Nov, holders vote on issuing "up to an aggregate of 1,128,272 shares" and "980,072 shares" on warrant exercise. One warrant was "adjusted... to $4.9757 per share following our reverse stock split" ([proxy](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1712762/000149315226048245/formdef14a.htm)). The proxy also says the current "liquidity position raises substantial doubt" about continuing (a repeat of earlier going-concern language).
- **Screen:**
  - $3.11; 20-day median $335k-474k a day.
  - "All Directors and Current Executive Officers as a Group (9 Individuals): 28,505 2.4 %". No 5% holder is listed.
  - Ongoing supply. Eligible.
- **Reaction:** $2.91 on 30 Oct, $2.99 on 2 Nov, $3.19 on 3 Nov, $3.11 on 4 Nov (intraday). The rise gives the ATM something to sell into.
- **Verdict:** **AVOID, public call #14.**
  - Invalidation: a close above **$3.96** (15 Oct, the last bounce).
  - Control: XBI. Review 3 Dec.
  - Evidence: `calls/evidence/14.csv` (15 quotes).

## 2. XRPN: an equity line goes live on an XRP treasury company

- **The supply:**
  - The resale S-1 for the Cantor equity line went effective on 30 Oct ("2026-10-30 16:00:00 S-1 ... Evernorth Holdings Inc."). It covers "up to 5,218,935 shares of the Class A common stock", priced at "ninety-seven percent (97%) of the VWAP".
  - That is about 20% of the "26,094,680 shares of Pubco Class A Common Stock" (arith) ([424B3](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2092592/000162828026068590/evernorth-424b3.htm)).
  - The bigger overhang is a separate S-1 for "up to 73,688,162 shares of Class A common stock", not yet effective ([S-1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2092592/000162828026068609/xrpn-20261030.htm)).
- **Screen:**
  - SBI holds 36.1%, the sponsor 18.0% and Chris Larsen 9.0%. Nobody is over 50%; the float outside the 5% holders is about 36.9% (arith).
  - $11.70 on 2 Nov; 20-day median $20.8M a day. Eligible.
  - It was already on the register, waiting to be re-read at EFFECT, and it has now been.
- **Reaction:** -17.6% on 2 Nov on 924,800 shares (3.7x the prior day) while XRP fell 0.5%. Supply, not the coin (inference). $11.24 on 4 Nov, from $15.27 a week earlier.
- **Risks to the call:**
  - It moves with XRP: a crypto rally could lift it whatever the supply.
  - The sponsor and Ripple lock-up runs to about 9 Apr 2027 (arith), so the 73.7M resale could take time to bite.
- **Verdict:** **AVOID, public call #15.**
  - Invalidation: a close above **$14.20** (30 Oct, the last close before the line went live).
  - Control: IBIT; there is no listed XRP fund on our data. Review 3 Dec.
  - Evidence: `calls/evidence/15.csv`.
