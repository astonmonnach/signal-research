## 1. DIBS: a billionaire's family keeps buying a $143M marketplace below his own cost

- **The filing:** [13D/A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1600641/000110465926116935/0001104659-26-116935-index.htm), filed 19 Oct.
  - United Refining (part of John Catsimatidis's Red Apple group) bought 449,500 shares on 15 Oct at $4.07.
  - The group now holds 2,635,385 shares, **7.8%**, up from 5.1% at its first 13D in Nov 2025.
  - Total cost $13.22M, an average of about **$5.01** a share (agent's arithmetic). It is buying more while under water.
- **Intent:** the first 13D says the stake is for investment and supports management and the company's $12M buyback. This amendment does not change that. It is a friendly accumulator, not an activist.
- **Update, same day (deep dive):** both buybacks are already spent. The $12M programme and a further $10M from May were used up by 30 Jun: $22.3M for 4.46M shares. No new authorisation has been filed. Catsimatidis is the next large buyer after the company stopped. See research/DIBS-2026-10-20.html.
- **The reaction:**
  - On 15 Oct the stock rose 8.4% on 654k shares; this buyer was about 69% of that volume (agent's arithmetic).
  - Since then it has been flat: $4.27 on 16 Oct, $4.25 on 19 Oct.
  - The 3-month range is $3.55-5.22.
- **Takeable:** yes ($4.25; median $360k a day).
- **Absences, the next checks:**
  - The latest 10-Q for cash and burn.
  - How much of the $12M buyback has been used.
  - Whether the group's buying is near a 10% line that brings Form 4 duty.
  - Any wire coverage.
- **What our tests say (description):**
  - IC1 found that clusters of insiders do not pay overall, but small companies did 7 points better than large ones.
  - This is one outside holder, not insiders. No test covers a single large holder accumulating below its cost, so it is a WATCH, not a call.
- **Verdict:** **WATCH.**
  - It moves up if the buying continues, the 10-Q shows a funded path, or the buyback is visibly working.
  - It moves down if the holder stops or sells, or cash is a problem.
