## 1. ESRT: the go-deeper checks on the Erez 13D

- **Who owns what** ([13D](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1541401/0000902664-26-004065-index.htm)):
  - The filing has three cover pages. Erez REIT Opportunities LP holds 7.5M shares (4.4%). **Erez Asset Management LLC and Bruce Schanzer hold 10.0M (5.8%)**, which is the group total. We quoted the smaller number on 6 Oct.
  - Item 3: about **$43M** from the funds' working capital, so roughly **$4.30 a share** including commissions (my arithmetic). Today's price is $4.45, so they're only about 3% up. They bought in the open market over the last 60 days (Annex A).
  - Item 6: no swaps, options or agreements with anyone else. So it's a single manager, not a group of funds acting together (as far as the filing shows).
- **Sum of the parts** (from the [Q2 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1541401/000154140126000033/esrt-20260630.htm)):
  - **Observatory:** Q2 revenue fell **28.5%** to $24.2M (from $33.9M). After the $14.8M of rent it pays the property side, its NOI was **-$2.3M** (+$3.4M a year ago). The 10-Q blames fewer visitors, weaker pass programmes and fewer international tourists. The company wrote off **$166.1M of goodwill**; $61.4M is still on the books and "remains at risk".
  - So the Observatory isn't hidden value right now. If anything, it's what is dragging the shares.
  - **Real estate** NOI was $105.0M in Q2; the group total was $102.6M.
- **Debt and value:**
  - Total debt principal is **$2.24bn**, and cash is $85.6M. Maturities: **$159M in 2027, $150M in 2028**, then $429M in 2029.
  - The 10-Q counts 172.2M Class A shares, 1.0M Class B and 110.5M OP units, about **283.6M units**. At $4.45 that's an equity value of **~$1.26bn**, so the enterprise value is about **$3.42bn**. Net debt is **1.7x** the equity value.
  - Q2 NOI x 4 = ~$411M, about a **12% yield on EV** before head-office costs. That's crude (the business is seasonal, and preferred units aren't counted), and it's **an inference, not a filed number**. Still, it's high for Manhattan property. It's the bull case Erez is pointing at.
- **Already-run test:** not run at all. ESRT is **-41% over 1 year and -39% over 3 years**, against VNQ +2% / +32%, SLG -14% / +63%, VNO -16% / +62% and BXP -17% / +27%.
- **Since the 13D** (2 Oct close to today): ESRT +6.8%, against VNQ -1.2%, SLG -1.7%, VNO -1.5% and BXP -2.9%. That's still about **+8 points ahead of its peers**.
- **What's missing:**
  - No company response: no 8-K and no press release since 5 Oct.
  - No Q3 results date in the filings I read.
  - No sign of a second holder joining.
- **Verdict:** **WATCH**, unchanged. The setup is real. A holder with 5.8% bought near today's price and is asking for a strategic review, and the property side looks cheap on a crude yield. But the Observatory is shrinking, and the debt (1.7x the equity) means any bad news hits the shares hard. What would move it to OWN: the company responds (talks, or a review announced), or a Q3 print shows Observatory visitors stabilising. It's already logged as `activist_13d` (+1) in the ledger, so there's no new call.

## Rules added today

- **13D holdings = the largest cover page.** A 13D has a cover page for each reporting person, and the first is usually the fund, the smallest. Quote the largest row-11 amount, which is the group total. Enforced by `watch/thirteen_d.py`, which prints every cover page and the group holding. (We quoted ESRT at 4.4% when the group holds 5.8%.)
