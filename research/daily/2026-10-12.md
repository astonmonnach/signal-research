## 2. SKYA: a planned $3.00 cash tender for 95% of the company

- **The release** ([GlobeNewswire, 11 Oct 23:14 New York time](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/12/3378548/0/en/skyai-announces-intention-to-commence-cash-tender-offer-to-purchase-common-stock-and-pre-funded-warrants-at-3-00-per-share-pre-funded-warrant.html)):
  - SkyAI says it **intends** to start a tender offer for up to 95% of each class of its shares and warrants at **$3.00 cash**. That is up to 46.37M shares and 20.72M warrants, about $201M in all.
  - It expects to start on or about 15 Oct and file the offer papers with the SEC by then. It expects the offer to run 10 business days.
  - It pays with cash and by selling Solana (SOL). It says it recently sold about 1M SOL, half its holding, and still holds about 1M.
  - In the same release the board rejected Forward Industries' latest unsolicited proposal of 0.306 Forward shares for each SkyAI share.
- **Conditions in the release:**
  - At least 50% of each class must be tendered.
  - SOL must close above $100 throughout the offer. SOL is $108.89 now, about 9% above the line. It was $120.79 on 6 Oct ([Yahoo](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/SOL-USD/history/)).
  - No big adverse change in stock or crypto markets, and no delisting as a result. SkyAI can waive any condition.
  - No financing condition.
  - If more than 95% of a class is tendered, that class is scaled back pro rata. The release mentions no odd-lot rule.
- **Price** ([SKYA prices](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/SKYA/history/)): $1.82 at Friday's close. It opened at $2.34 today and is about $2.58 now, on about 35M shares against under 0.5M on a normal day. The gap to $3.00 is about 16%.
  - Forward's rejected proposal is worth about $2.26 a share at Forward's $7.40 ([Yahoo](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/FWDI/history/); my arithmetic).
  - If the offer failed and the stock went back to $1.82, that is a 29% loss. So the price implies roughly a one in three chance it fails (my arithmetic, and crude).
- **Who else is in it:** a group led by Bastion Trading owns over 10% and bought 44,000 shares at $1.78 on 7 Oct under a pre-arranged plan ([Form 4](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1737995/000092189526002775/0000921895-26-002775-index.htm)).
- **What's missing:**
  - The offer has not started and the release says it may not. There is no SC TO-I yet, so no firm expiry, no final conditions and no odd-lot terms.
  - Ten business days is shorter than the 20 business days an issuer tender normally has to stay open. That is my recollection of the rule, not checked today. [UNVERIFIED] The papers will settle it.
  - I haven't read SkyAI's 10-Q, so I can't say what its cash and SOL are worth per share.
  - Exchange status: no delisting notice in its filings since 15 Jul. I did not look further back.
- **Tender rule check** (LESSONS, 10 Oct): price $3.00; cap 95% of each class; expiry not set (about 10 business days from about 15 Oct); odd-lot rule none stated. The 15 Oct date is on the calendar.
- **Linked names:** FWDI (Forward Industries, the rejected bidder).
- **Verdict:** **WATCH.** A 16% gap is far above the 3.5% fee hurdle, and a 95% cap means very little scaling back. But today it is a press release, not an offer, and one bad week for Solana ends it. Read the SC TO-I the day it is filed and decide then. Logged as `issuer_tender_intent`, direction 0.

## Rules added today

- **A code-P line is never judged alone.** Open the company's Form 4 list for the last 30 days. Two or more different insiders buying in 30 days is a cluster. The scanner's "Clusters" line only looks at one day, and it said "none" for Borr. Enforced by hand in the research run; not yet in code (LESSONS.md, 12 Oct).
- **In the Activists section, the company shown must be the one held.** The older section lists every company named on a 13D, so the filer can look like the target (ASPI, WKEY). Read the 13D before giving it a line. Enforced by hand in the triage; not yet in code (LESSONS.md, 12 Oct).
