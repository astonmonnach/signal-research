## 1. GPRO: a restatement turns into loan defaults while a takeover is pending

- **The filing:** [8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1500435/000150043526000064/0001500435-26-000064-index.htm), items 2.02, 4.02 and 8.01. Accepted 17:30 on Fri 16 Oct, after the close.
- **The restatement (Item 4.02):**
  - FY2025, Q3 2025, Q1 2026 and Q2 2026 are to be restated.
  - The causes are errors valuing the Mateo Financing warrants and the embedded derivative in the YA II convertibles.
  - It is non-cash and small in size: the FY2025 net loss goes from $93.5M to $92.6M.
  - There is a material weakness. The company says there was no fraud.
  - It was found while preparing the merger proxy.
- **The defaults (Item 8.01):**
  - The restatement is a default under the Wells Fargo revolver, the Farallon term loan, the YA II debentures and the notes held by Woodman's affiliates.
  - On 16 Oct the term lenders also waived a missed asset-coverage ratio at 30 Sep and 31 Dec 2025.
  - The company "cannot assure" it will get the remaining waivers.
- **The takeover** ([1 Sep 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1500435/000162828026060181/gpro-20260901.htm)):
  - Action Acquisitions LLC pays **$1.14 cash**, which can be cut if working capital falls short, plus **0.1 share** of the surviving company.
  - It is funded by an equity commitment from Midtown Equities LLC.
  - Outside date **31 Dec 2026**. The proxy is not filed yet.
  - The Woodman Family Trust (the chief executive's) holds 20.3% ([13D](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1500435/000110465926106446/)).
- **Price** (all before the filing):
  - $1.15 on 15 Oct, $1.05 on 16 Oct (-8.7% on 1.2 times normal volume; IWM -1.7%).
  - The cash part alone is 8.6% above $1.05 (agent's arithmetic).
  - Against about $0.60 before the deal (28 Aug), the price implies closing odds of at most about 83%, ignoring the stub share (agent's arithmetic).
- **Takeable:** yes ($1.05; median $12.1M a day). It sits close to the $1 floor.
- **Absences:**
  - No wire story found.
  - The merger agreement's walk-away rights around a restatement or a lender default are not read.
  - The working-capital adjustment's size is not known.
- **Linked names:** Action Acquisitions and Midtown Equities (both private). Same industry code 3861, a weak link: IMAX, KODK and MITQ.
- **Verdict:** **tracked, no call.**
  - The gap looks attractive, but the parts that matter are unread or unknowable from outside: the walk-away rights, the cash cut and the remaining waivers.
  - Monday's open shows how the market reads it. On the calendar: 31 Dec outside date.

## 3. SKYA: the tender launched, with no SOL condition and a tax catch for UK holders

- **The filing:** [SC TO-I](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1737995/000093041326003076/0000930413-26-003076-index.htm), accepted 13:20 on 16 Oct, about 40 minutes after the 16 Oct run checked EDGAR. The [Offer to Purchase](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1737995/000093041326003076/c117899_ex-a1a.htm) is dated 15 Oct.
- **Terms:**
  - $3.00 for up to 48,311,489 shares (95%) and 18,775,877 warrants (95% of each class, less $0.0001 per warrant). Up to $201.26M in all.
  - Pro rata within each class, with **no odd-lot priority**.
  - The minimum is 50% per class, but the two pre-funded warrant classes count together, and the share wording is ambiguous.
  - The listed SKYAW warrants are not included.
- **Conditions:**
  - **No SOL price condition.** The $100 SOL condition was in the 9 Oct release only. The company sold all its SOL on 15 Oct and holds about $223.8M of cash.
  - The rest are standard, all judged "in our reasonable judgment": material adverse change, legal action, a competing proposal, any new holder over 5%, and a delisting risk that the company decides itself.
- **Expiry:** one minute after 11:59 pm New York on **Thu 29 Oct**. That is 10 business days. US tender rules require 20, so an extension to about 12 or 13 Nov is likely (inference, not from the filing).
- **Tax, the point that matters for this account:**
  - The company "intends to treat such payment as a dividend distribution for withholding purposes". Non-US holders face withholding "at a rate of 30% of the gross proceeds" unless they file a W-8 form claiming a lower rate.
  - At a 15% UK treaty rate (inference), $3.00 nets about $2.55, below the $2.65 close.
  - Any excess withheld comes back only through an IRS refund claim.
- **Price:** $2.74 (15 Oct), $2.65 (16 Oct). The gross gap is 13.2%.
- **Holders:** Bastion (Wei Zhu) 8.4%; Solios/FalconX 9.9%; Electric Capital 6.1% and Saba 5.99% (both passive). The Offer to Purchase does not mention Forward Industries or Bastion's challenge.
- **Verdict:** **tracked, no call.** The offer is real and cash-funded. But a UK holder who tenders gets less than the market price unless the withheld tax is reclaimed. The 16 Oct note has a correction, and the rule is in LESSONS (18 Oct).
