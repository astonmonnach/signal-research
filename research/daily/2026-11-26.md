## 1. MNTS: a poison pill against the buyer (UP call #19)
- **The filing:** "On November 24, 2026, the Board of Directors (the "Board") of Momentus Inc." adopted a rights plan. The trigger is 10%: an "Acquiring Person" is anyone who "Beneficially Owns 10% or more of the outstanding Common Shares". The release went out at 01:01 UTC on 25 Nov, before that day's open.
- **Burkle is named:** "Ronald Wayne Burkle, OA3, LLC, Kenneth James Abdalla and Warrior Global Holdings, LLC ... are deemed a single group, and ... are expressly Grandfathered Stockholders". But "A Grandfathered Stockholder that subsequently acquires Beneficial Ownership of additional Common Shares becomes an Acquiring Person unless, upon that acquisition, it Beneficially Owns less than 10%". The group reported 12.9%, so any further purchase triggers it.
- **What a trigger means:** every other holder could get shares "having a value equal to twice the applicable exercise price", which would dilute the buyer heavily. Record date 4 Dec; the rights expire 24 Nov 2027.
- **What's left:** "It does not prevent the Board from considering an acquisition proposal or redeeming the Rights before any Person becomes an Acquiring Person." So the route now is a proposal or a board fight, not open-market buying (inference). The board chose to block the buyer days after refusing his $1.86 block, which reads as resistance (inference).
- **Price:** $2.05 on 23 Nov, $2.04 on 24 Nov, $2.02 on 25 Nov; the stock barely moved on the pill.
- **Verdict:** UP call #19 runs unchanged under UP1; the record entry is the 25 Nov open ($2.04), and on X it counts from Friday's open. The pill is recorded, not used to override the rule. T026's "more purchases" trigger is now blocked; an Item 4 change or a proposal is what's left (note added). Evidence: research/evidence/2026-11-26-MNTS.csv (8 rows, 0 problems). Calendar: 4 Dec record date.
- **Linked names:** MNTS -- fund:OA3 (Burkle) (rights plan link added).
