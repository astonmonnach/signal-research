## 1. ACB: a board-backed deal, but the spread doesn't pay for the risk

- **The deal** ([material change report](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1683541/000110465926122357/tm2628851d1_ex99-1.htm)):
  - Each Aurora share can elect C$7.80 in cash or "5.8626 common shares in the capital of Organigram". Proration caps the totals at "no more than 205,826,172 Organigram Shares and no more than C$253,638,772 in cash".
  - In practice, everyone gets "C$3.75 in cash plus 3.0431 Organigram Shares per Aurora Share". Holders who don't elect "shall be deemed to have elected to receive the Combination Elected Consideration".
  - The C$7.80 headline assumes Organigram at C$1.33.
- **What has to happen:**
  - Aurora holders: a two-thirds vote. Organigram holders: a majority vote "excluding votes held by BAT".
  - A BC court plan of arrangement, the Competition Act (Canada), and German foreign-investment approval.
  - There is no financing condition; BAT funds the cash at C$1.33 a share.
  - Closing "may not occur earlier than January 6, 2027"; the outside date is 2 May 2027.
  - Aurora pays a $25.2M fee if it walks.
- **Curaleaf's rival bid** is "0.4013 of a Curaleaf Holdings, Inc. subordinate voting share plus US$1.00 in cash". That's worth about US$4.92 (arith), below ACB's price, and it expires 4 Dec. Curaleaf says the deal "blocks any chance of an enhanced Curaleaf bid".

- **For a UK holder:** listed Aurora shares are "generally" not taxable Canadian property for non-residents. The tax-deferred rollover is for Canadian residents only. Selling ACB before closing avoids getting OGI shares, which trade under US$1 on Nasdaq, at $0.952.
- **Verdict:** **tracked, no call. T006 fired 3 Nov; T021 replaces it.**
  - The trigger: by 31 Dec, both votes pass and ACB still trades 6%+ below the package the next day. Then re-read it as OWN.
  - Until then the trade needs a hedge (short OGI) that a cash account can't do.

## 2. MTUS (open position): the Q4 guide is what the market sold

- **Q3** ([release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1598428/000119312526443736/mtus-ex99_1.htm)):
  - Sales $337.2M (+10% on a year ago); adjusted EBITDA $34.5M.
  - "We are pleased to have achieved our annualized $250 million run-rate sales target for aerospace & defense this quarter".
  - The 10-Q shows aerospace and defence at $66.4M against $47.2M a year earlier. The DLA's initial "delivery order valued at approximately $125.0 million" has "up to 24 months" to fulfil.
- **The guide:**
  - "we expect full-year adjusted EBITDA for 2026 to be between $100 to $105 million". After nine months of $88.1M, that leaves **Q4 at about $12-17M against Q3's $34.5M** (arith).
  - Reasons given: a maintenance shutdown ($10-15M), shipments down a high-single-digit percentage, and slightly lower price/mix. The company calls it seasonal.
- **Insiders:** the two officers' Form 144s (22,800 and 9,092 shares at about $22) were under 10b5-1 plans adopted on 12 and 19 May. Pre-planned, not a reaction.
- **Reaction:** $21.32 (2 Nov), $19.69 (3 Nov, -7.6%), about $17.3 intraday on 4 Nov.
- **The rule:** exit on a daily close below $17.00. The defence part of the thesis is intact; the near-term earnings case is weaker. The close decides.
