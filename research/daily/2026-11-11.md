## 1. IMXI: Western Union walks, Intermex says no
- **What happened:**
  - The 10-Q (9 Nov) states the deal terms: holders were to receive "$16.00 per share in cash". Either party could terminate "if the Merger is not completed by November 10, 2026".
  - Western Union's release (Business Wire, 10 Nov, after the close): California's DFPI delivered "unacceptable final Conditions of Approval" on the evening of 10 Nov. The board terminated, and "neither Western Union nor Intermex is required to make any payment to the other".
  - Intermex's reply (GlobeNewswire, 11 Nov 06:30 ET): it rejects "Western Union's purported termination". It "has satisfied all of its obligations", and reserves "its rights to specific performance and to hold Western Union liable for substantial damages". Western Union's notice "followed its receipt on November 10, 2026 of the conditional approval of the Merger from the California Department of Financial Protection and Innovation".
  - Evidence: `research/evidence/2026-11-11-IMXI.csv` (0 problems). The two release quotes were read in the browser because both newswires return 403 to the checker. Re-check them against the 8-Ks, due within four business days.
- **Price:** $8.35 on 4 Nov when we called it binary. $7.57 on 9 Nov, then $6.495 on 10 Nov (-14.2%, the outside-date day, before the notice). About $6.34 intraday on 11 Nov (-2.3%). The pre-deal price was $9.28. Liquid ($5.2M a day).
- **What we don't know:** whether the merger agreement lets Western Union refuse conditions like these (the "burdensome condition" wording was not read today), and how long a Delaware fight would take.
- **Bet before the news (rule 2026-11-04):** at $6.34 the payoff is about +152% if a court orders the deal at $16. The loss case is the stock without a deal, which already trades about 32% below its pre-deal $9.28. We have no basis for the odds of winning specific performance. That makes it a legal bet on a contract we have not read, not a trade. Re-read it when the 8-Ks, the merger agreement's termination section or any complaint are filed.
- **Verdict:** **tracked, no call.** The 4 Nov "binary, no trade" call was right on price: $8.35 to $6.50 before the termination. It is not a numbered call, so it is never counted as a win.
