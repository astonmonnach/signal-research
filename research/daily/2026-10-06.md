# Research: Tue 6 Oct 2026

**Headline:** two big takeovers signed on Sun 4 Oct show the same pattern. The buyer's shares dropped hard, the target jumped
close to the offer, and peers re-rated around it. Neither leaves a long trade worth taking yet. ESRT's new holder is the one
**takeable** lead, and it needs a deep dive before any verdict.

Sources:
- SEC filings (linked below) and C.H. Robinson's press release (8-K exhibit 99.1).
- Yahoo prices: closes for Fri 2 and Mon 5 Oct, and intraday for 6 Oct at about 19:45 UK.

Controls are SPY, IWM, the software ETF IGV and the REIT ETF VNQ.

## 1. Schneider Electric buys PTC: $205 cash

- **The deal** ([PTC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/857005/000119312526413124/d174191d8k.htm), signed 4 Oct):
  - $205 in cash per share.
  - Needs PTC's shareholder vote, plus antitrust (HSR) and **foreign-investment** clearances. Schneider is French.
  - PTC pays a $700M break fee if it takes a better offer.
  - Debt financing is committed (Morgan Stanley Europe, Société Générale).
- **The reaction:** PTC went from $144.03 to $193.03 (+34.0%). **Schneider's ADR (SBGSY) fell 14.9%.**
- **The spread:** $205 vs $193.03 is a **6.2% gross** gap.
  - If the deal broke, PTC would probably go back towards its pre-deal $144.
  - The price therefore implies about a **10% chance of failure** [computed: $205 discounted at 4% for ~10 months ≈ $198.5; (198.5 − 193.0) / (198.5 − 144.0) ≈ 10%].
  - The close date isn't in the 8-K, and the foreign-investment review is the open risk.
- **Second order: peers re-rated on the takeover price.**
  - Bentley (BSY) +10.2%, Autodesk (ADSK) +8.1%, Procore (PCOR) +5.8%, Dassault (DASTY) +4.4%.
  - Over the same days IGV was +2.3% and SPY +1.3%.
  - This is the usual "who's next" re-rating. It happened within two days, so it **has already run**.
- **Verdict:**
  - PTC is a merger-arbitrage position (6.2% for perhaps 10–12 months, with roughly −25% if it breaks), not a research edge. **Tracked, no call.**
  - The peers have already run.

## 2. C.H. Robinson buys RXO: $17.25 cash + 0.0856 CHRW shares

- **The deal** ([CHRW 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1043277/000119312526413203/d115124d8k.htm), signed 4 Oct):
  - The consideration was worth $30.25 a share at signing (with CHRW at $151.87), 29% above RXO's Friday close. The deal is valued at $5.8bn.
  - It's about 57% cash and 43% CHRW shares, and RXO holders end up owning 11% of the combined company.
  - It's expected to close in H1 2027, after regulatory approval and RXO's shareholder vote.
  - MFN Partners (~17%) signed a voting agreement. That's the [RXO 13D](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1929561/0001193125-26-414547-index.htm), which is **not** activism.
  - CHRW expects $300M of cost savings and is **pausing share buybacks**.
- **The reaction:** RXO went from $23.38 to $28.62 (+22.4%). **CHRW went from $157.72 to $137.00 (−13.1%)**, on 8.7M shares Monday.
- **The spread:**
  - Because 43% is paid in CHRW stock, CHRW's fall cut the deal's value to **$28.98**.
  - Against RXO at $28.62, that's a **1.25% gross** gap, about 1.7% a year to mid-2027. That's below the return on cash, so there's no edge.
- **Second order:** rival brokers fell. HUBG −5.6%, JBHT −3.3%, LSTR −2.1%, XPO −1.1%, while SPY was +1.3%.
  - My read: a bigger number-one broker is a tougher competitor. That's **an inference, not shown by any filing.**
  - Antitrust scope and market shares aren't given in the release, and I haven't checked them.
- **Verdict:** no trade. The spread is too thin, and we can't short CHRW. Tracked.

**The pattern across both deals:** the buyer fell 13–15% within two days. If it keeps happening, that's testable. Every buyer
and peer from today is now logged in the ledger (`deal_acquirer`, `deal_peer`, `merger_arb`, tracked only).

## 3. ESRT: a new holder pushing for a strategic review, the takeable lead

- **The filing** ([ESRT 13D](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1541401/0000902664-26-004065-index.htm), filed 5 Oct):
  - Erez REIT Opportunities LP / Erez Asset Management own **7.5M shares, 4.4%**.
  - Item 4 says the shares are **undervalued**. They've talked to management and the board about capital allocation and "**a potential strategic review of some or all of the Issuer's assets**".
  - They may also raise board composition, buybacks and the dividend.
- **The reaction:** ESRT went from $4.17 to $4.43 (+6.4%). Office REITs fell over the same days (SLG −1.9%, VNO −1.5%, BXP −1.9%), and VNQ was +0.3%. That's about **+8 points against its peers**, on about $33M a day of trading.
- **It's takeable:** long, liquid and over $1. It's logged as `activist_13d` (+1) from today's open.
- **Still to check tomorrow** (the go-deeper list):
  1. Sum of the parts: what the Observatory segment's profit (from the 10-Q) and the property portfolio are worth against the market value.
  2. Debt and maturities.
  3. Whether Erez is part of a bigger group.
  4. How the company responds.
  5. The "already run" test: the 1-year and 3-year moves against VNQ.
- **Verdict:** WATCH until that's done.
