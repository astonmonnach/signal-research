## 1. KNOP: the sponsor pays the activist $12.50 for a $7.32 unit
- **The filing:** "On November 20, 2026, KNOT entered into a Unit Purchase Agreement with Astaris to purchase 2,789,070 Common Units for a cash purchase price of $12.50 per Common Unit or $34,863,375 in the aggregate" (KNOT 13D/A, 20 Nov, filed after the close). $12.50 is 70.8% above Friday's $7.32 close (arith).
- **The sponsor's stake:** 16,789,984 units, "44.3" percent of "37,909,633 Common Units outstanding", including "4,249,291 Common Units" from its convertible preferred.
- **What it says it may do:** acquisitions "could be effectuated through open market purchases, tender or exchange offers, exercise of the limited call right contained in the Partnership Agreement, negotiated merger transactions".
- **The call right:** it needs "more than 80% of the Common Units", priced at the greater of a 20-day average and "the highest price paid by the General Partner or any of its affiliates for Common Units during the 90-day period preceding the date such notice is first mailed". So until about 18 Feb 2027 (arith) any call notice would be priced at $12.50 or more. KNOT is at 44.3%, a long way from 80%.
- **History:** KNOT proposed "$10 per Common Unit in cash" in 2025, and in March 2026 the parties "terminated discussions regarding the Proposal". Astaris has now accepted a standstill "for a period of 24 months".
- **Inference (not in a filing):** paying one holder $12.50 while the market price is $7.32 means KNOT either values the units above $12.50 or paid a premium to remove the activist. The standstill also removes the outside pressure, which cuts both ways.
- **What we don't know:** why the units fell 29.1% in a month, from a $10.32 high to $7.32 (arith). No filing read explains it. Q3 results have not been filed.
- **The bet before the news (rule 2026-11-04):** the trigger is the headline event itself (a tender, a merger proposal or a call notice), so here is the sizing.
  - Entry would be Monday's open; Friday's $7.32 is the unaffected price.
  - Upside to $10.00 is +36.6%; to $12.50 it's +70.8% (arith).
  - With an assumed 20% downside if nothing happens, breaking even needs about 35% odds of a $10 offer, or about 22% odds of $12.50 (arith).
- **Price:** $7.32, $757k a day.
- **Verdict:** **WATCH, T024** (deadline 18 Feb 2027). A long would be the trader's call, so it is not a public call. Annual meeting 22 Dec, record date 27 Nov (calendar). Evidence: research/evidence/2026-11-21-KNOP.csv (12 rows, 0 problems).
- **Linked names:** KNOP -- fund:KNOT and KNOP -- fund:Astaris (added today). No listed names within 3 links. Same industry code (water transport): FLNG, GLNG, NAT, TNK, INSW and others, a weak link.
