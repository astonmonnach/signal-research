## 2. SSTI: the CVR is priced at about $0.40; management's own case pays $1.05

- **The offer:** [Offer to Purchase](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1351636/000114036126040176/ny20082824x1_exa1a.htm) from Transom, filed 20 Oct.
  - $8.00 cash plus one non-transferable CVR.
  - The CVR pays on FY2027 ShotSpotter plus SafePointe revenue: **$0.50 at $73.5M, then $0.05 per step, up to $3.00 at $87M.**
  - Expires one minute after 11:59 pm New York on **17 Nov**.
  - Over half the shares must be tendered. No financing condition; not reportable under HSR. Holders of 15.8% (Veradace) and 17.0% (Lauder) have signed tender agreements.
- **What is new:** the [14D-9](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1351636/000114036126040184/ny20082880x1_sc14d9.htm) of 20 Oct.
  - Management's risk-adjusted forecast is about **$77.4M** of FY2027 ShotSpotter and SafePointe revenue, which "would result in a payment of $1.05 per CVR".
  - The adviser, Tidal Partners, discounted that at about 16.5% to **about $0.77** today.
  - The forecasts were "based on the management plan provided in August 2026 following the Company's second-quarter earnings results".
- **Price:** $8.40 (+1.45% on 20 Oct), up 2.3% since the deal day (29 Sep, $8.21). The market pays about $0.40 for the CVR (arith).
- **The payoff per share at $8.40 (arith):**
  - $8.00 comes back when the tender closes. After that only $0.40 is at risk until the CVR pays (no earlier than about mid-2028; inference from FY2027 plus the payment period).
  - CVR pays nothing: -$0.40 (-4.8%).
  - Management's case: +$0.65 (+7.7%), or about 2.6x on the $0.40 at risk.
  - Full payout: +$2.60 (+31%).
- **Against it:**
  - The buyer told the board that customer renewals and the pipeline were "less favorable than originally thought".
  - In Nov 2025 the company cut its revenue guidance by 7%, to $104.0M, citing Puerto Rico's renewal, CrimeTracer bookings and execution issues.
  - The forecast is management's own, made while the company was being sold.
- **For a UK holder:** the $8.00 is a sale, so nothing is withheld. Whether a UK broker credits a non-transferable CVR, and how its payment is taxed, is still to check. A reading agent says only the imputed-interest part is withheld; not verified.
- **Takeable:** yes ($538k a day).
- **Linked names:** Transom Capital (buyer).
- **Verdict:** **tracked, with a trigger (T010).** The CVR's value rests on one segment's revenue, which we have not yet checked in the filings.
  - **Re-read for WATCH** if the Q3 10-Q, or any filing before the 17 Nov expiry, shows ShotSpotter and SafePointe revenue on a path to $73.5M or more for FY2027.
  - Any buy has to be made before 17 Nov.
