## 1. CPAC: Holcim takes Cementos Pacasmayo private, and the spread is small but nearly certain

  - Expiry is **4pm New York on 20 Nov**, with payment two trading days later.
  - There is **no minimum and no financing condition**. The only conditions are that the Peru offer is not suspended by the regulator or a court, and that no order blocks either offer.
  - Afterwards Holcim plans to delist from the NYSE and deregister.
  - Holcim's 13D/A No. 3 confirms the start ([13D/A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1221029/000110465926117872/0001104659-26-117872-index.htm)).
- **For a UK holder:**
  - ADSs tendered in the US offer are not Peruvian-source income, so no Peruvian tax is due. Holcim pays the $0.05 per ADS cancellation fee.
  - This is a third-party cash offer, so there is no US dividend withholding (reader's reading; to confirm with the broker).
- **The spread (arith):**
  - $12.66 close: 2.5% gross. $12.79 intraday on 22 Oct: about 1.5%.
  - It is about 33 days to payment. Annualised, that is roughly 28% or 17% (arith).
  - The spread moves with the PEN/USD rate.
  - Implied completion is about 95% against the $6.99 price before the deal.
- **Costs at this account's size (arith):** the ~3.5% round-trip cost on a £40-50 position is bigger than the spread. This trade only works with a larger position or a dollar balance already held in the account.
- **TF1:** passes. It is 0.6% below the 52-week high and above the 50-day average. But this is a deal-pinned arb, which TF1's primary test leaves out.
- **Takeable:** yes, but thin: median $190k a day, and only $120k traded on 21 Oct.
- **Linked names:** Holcim (buyer).
- **Absences:**
  - IBKR's own cut-off for tendering ADSs, and whether a UK client can tender into the US offer.
  - What happens to holders who do not tender, after the delisting.
- **Verdict:** **WATCH.**
  - **Trigger (T011, by 18 Nov):** IBKR confirms a UK account can tender into the US offer before its cut-off, with the spread after all costs still positive.
