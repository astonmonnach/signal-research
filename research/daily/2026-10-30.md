## 1. SEER: bids rejected, stock back at half its net cash

- **What happened.** On 29 Oct, after the close (EDGAR accepted the 8-K at 16:41 ET), the committee said it "has concluded its review process and decided to reject the recent acquisition proposals made to Seer" ([release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1726445/000119312526437906/d178905dex991.htm)). The two bids were:
  - Radoff-JEC: "$2.55 per share in cash plus a contingent value right".
  - The CEO, Omid Farokhzad: "$2.45 per share in cash plus two separate contingent value rights".
- **The committee's answer:** "Seer to Remain an Independent Public Company", because both bids "fail to reflect Seer's intrinsic value". **It said nothing about a buyback, tender, special dividend or liquidation.**
- **The cash** (Q2 10-Q, $ thousands):
  - Cash 26,075, short-term investments 126,778, long-term investments 56,631; total liabilities 30,958, of which 22,390 are leases.
  - Net cash $178.5M, or **$3.22 a share** on 55,372,015 shares (arith). There is no Class B left; it converted in Dec 2025.
  - Burn: $25.0M of operating cash in six months (15.4M in Q1, 9.6M in Q2, arith). That puts net cash at about $3.00 a share at 30 Sep (arith, inference).

- **Reaction:** $1.94 on 29 Oct (-2.5%, before the news). $1.68 on 30 Oct (**-13.4%** vs IWM -1.6%). From 30 Jun to 30 Oct: +1.2% vs IWM -7.0%. Takeable: the 20-day median is $294k a day.
- **Why it might not close:**
  - The CEO holds 12.0% and runs the company. SoftBank holds 9.3%, aMoon 9.0% and Radoff-JEC 7.8%.
  - A 4.9% tax-benefit pill blocks new large holders until 25 Feb 2027. Holders did not ratify it.
  - The CFO resigned on 1 Oct.
  - There is $25.1M of buyback authority, but only 202,979 shares were bought in Q2.
- **Peers** (arith from their 10-Qs):
  - QSI and NAUT re-rated from about 1x cash on sector momentum, to 2.0x and 4.2x.
  - LAB's discount widened to 0.52x once its cash went into a reverse merger.
  - Without a cash return, SEER looks more like LAB (inference).
- **Lab note:** SEER is the control stock on public AVOID #7 (NAUT). SEER's company-specific drop now flatters that comparison. Read #7 against IWM, its benchmark, not the control.
- **Verdict:** **WATCH, T019.**
  - Trigger: by **30 Nov** Seer files an 8-K, SC TO-I or its Q3 10-Q announcing a tender offer, special dividend or other cash return of **$25M or more** (about $0.45 a share).
  - Re-read at once on a new 13D/A from Radoff-JEC or the CEO.
  - Q3 results come on **10 Nov** after the close. That source is a third-party copy of the company's release; the company site blocked automated reads.
