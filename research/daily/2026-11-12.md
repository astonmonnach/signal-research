## 1. ICMB / BCIC: a bid paid in a discounted currency
- **What happened** (8-K and joint release, filed 12 Nov; the release went out 11 Nov 6pm ET, after the close):
  - BCIC "entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger" with ICMB. It needs only ICMB holders' approval ("subject to approval by ICMB stockholders"), and holders of "approximately 41% of ICMB's shares outstanding" have signed voting agreements. Those agreements also carry "transfer restriction, and standstill provisions ... prior to and after the consummation of the Mergers".
  - Terms: "ICMB stockholders will receive aggregate consideration equal to 90% of ICMB's net asset value at closing". It "will be paid in BCIC shares, valued at 100% of BCIC's net asset value per share at closing, with the number of shares issued capped at 19.99% of BCIC's common stock outstanding at signing. Any remaining consideration will be paid in cash."
  - Using adjusted NAVs of "ICMB ($2.76 per share) and BCIC ($13.00 per share)", the release values ICMB "at approximately $2.49 per share" at NAV and "at approximately $1.38 per share" at BCIC's $6.57 close of 9 Nov.
  - ICMB's debt must be paid off at closing ("the payoff of ICMB's outstanding indebtedness"). The break fee is "$1,309,460.46". Expected close: "the first calendar quarter of 2027".
- **The arithmetic (ours):**
  - The share cap binds. 19.99% of BCIC's 12,387,452 shares is 2,476,252 new shares for 14,432,472 ICMB shares: **0.1716 BCIC shares plus about $0.26 cash per ICMB share**. Check: at $6.57 that is $1.39 against the release's $1.38.
  - At BCIC's $5.535 (13:56 ET today) the package is **about $1.21**. ICMB at $1.00 sits 17% below it.
  - About $0.95 of the $1.21 is BCIC stock. So a holder of ICMB mostly holds BCIC at a discount, plus a cash stub.
- **Why the gap is there (inference):**
  - **BCIC itself is falling.** NAV is $13.13 at 30 Sep against $16.68 at 31 Dec. Net assets fell from $179.5M to $162.7M in Q3 alone (-9.4%, arith). Asset coverage is 156.95% against the 150% legal minimum. It opened $5.50 (-16.3%) today and trades at 42% of NAV (arith).
  - **The cash is the residual.** If ICMB's NAV keeps sliding before closing (from $4.03 at the start of 2026 to $3.02 at 30 Sep), the cash part shrinks first. A $500,000 holdback comes out of it too.
  - **ICMB is a forced seller.** Its asset coverage is 1.42, under the 150% a BDC needs to add debt ("at least equal to 150% immediately after each such issuance"). It paid no dividend in the first nine months of 2026, and its adviser waived $1.8M of base fees in nine months.
  - **Time and hedging.** The close is four or more months out, and a UK cash account can't short BCIC to lock the gap.
- **Reaction (actual times):** ICMB closed $0.618 on 11 Nov. It opened $0.999, hit $1.10 in the first half hour (45.7M shares in that half hour), and was $1.00 at 13:56 ET on 61.7M shares, 4.3x its share count. BCIC opened $5.50 and traded $5.36-5.73. The whole move was in the opening gap: the news came after the close, as 95% of flagged filings do.
- **Supply note:** up to 2,476,252 new BCIC shares (19.99%) go to ICMB holders at closing. About 41% of them go to holders who are bound by transfer restrictions; the rest could be sold (inference). It is a one-off share issue priced at NAV, so it is not a public AVOID under the 27 Oct rules.
- **Verdict:** **tracked, no call.** ICMB isn't takeable on its normal volume (20-day median about $40k a day before today). Today's frenzy was one day of volume, and it won't change the rule. BCIC has no buy signal: the acquirer pays in its own discounted stock while its NAV falls. This is not the cash-bid kind of "outside buyer" from the review lead. Re-read at the proxy (vote date) and at BCIC's next NAV.
- **Linked names:** BCIC (merger counterparty). The ICMB key holders include Investcorp BDC Holdings. BCIC's CEO calls it "another step" in "driving consolidation across the BDC sector". Other small discounted BDCs may be approached (inference; no sourced link).
