## 1. CCAP: a $75M bid for a $340M fund at half of NAV
- **What was filed** (results 8-K, accepted 9 Nov 16:10 ET, after the close; evidence: `research/evidence/2026-11-10-CCAP.csv`, 0 problems):
  - "On November 5, 2026, the Board authorized a share repurchase program of up to $50 million of the Company's common stock."
  - "Separately, Sun Life plans to purchase up to an additional $25 million of the Company's common stock in the open market through an independent third-party broker pursuant to a Rule 10b5-1 trading plan." Sun Life is the adviser's parent.
  - The company's own buying will also run through "an independent third-party broker pursuant to a Rule 10b5-1 trading plan".
  - "Net asset value (NAV) per share was $17.72 at September 30, 2026", down from $19.28 a year earlier. Net investment income was $0.37 a share for the quarter. "The Company's net debt-to-equity ratio (3) was 1.36x".
- **Size (arith):** $75M against about $340M of market value at $9.24 (36.8M shares) is about 22%. The price is 52% of NAV.
- **Reaction so far:** none in regular hours. 5 Nov to 9 Nov: CCAP -2.2%, against IWM +4.3%, the BDC ETF BIZD +0.7%, and peers WHF -1.7% and TCPC -3.5%. One month: CCAP +1.7%, BIZD +4.7%. After hours on 9 Nov: about $9.50 (+2.8%) on thin data. Liquidity: 20-day median about $1.68M a day, so takeable.
- **Why it may be unnoticed:** a mid-size BDC's results release, after the close; the flow is buried under "Recent Developments".
- **What we don't know:** the 10b5-1 plans' price limits and pace are not disclosed, and "up to" is not a commitment. A 48% discount to NAV says the market doubts the marks (inference: NAV is down 8% in a year, and peers WHF and TCPC also trade at deep discounts). Dividends are dividend-treated for a UK holder; check how much is exempt interest income before counting the yield (the VVR lesson).
- **Bet before the news (rule 2026-11-04):** the news is already out, so the question is the flow. Our trigger is not the headline: it is a filing that shows the buying under way while the price is still under 60% of NAV.
- **Verdict:** **WATCH**, T023. Trigger: by 31 Jan 2027, a filing shows the announced buying under way (a CCAP 8-K, 10-Q or 10-K reporting repurchases, or a Sun Life Form 4 or 13D/A showing purchases), while CCAP closes at or below $10.63 (60% of NAV). Added to the watchlist; IBKR change pending.
- **Linked names:** CCAP to Sun Life (counterparty, adviser's parent). No listed name within 3 links, and no industry code in sic_map.csv.

## Rules added today
- **2026-11-10 (ELMT):** an SF 1449's "does not obligate funds" is form boilerplate, and a guaranteed IDIQ minimum is money the government must order. Never say an award is unfunded or has no orders on that sentence. Enforced: `calls/check_evidence.py` (`unfunded_claim`) plus tests; skill step 6b.
- **2026-11-10 (scanner):** a code-P row counts as an insider buy only when the security is common stock and the footnotes don't say offering, from the company or dividend reinvestment. A same-day gift offsets a buy, and one joint purchase filed twice is one buyer. Six false positives on 6-9 Nov (OBIO, ANVS, FFAI, APOG, LKFN, SYNX). Enforced: `watch/scan_market.py` plus `tests/test_scan_insider_buys.py`.
