## 1. VVR: Saba buys 5% of a loan fund at a 12.5% discount

- **The filing** ([13D, 4 Nov](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1059386/000106299326005733/xslSCHEDULE_13D_X02/primary_doc.xml)):
  - "Percent of class represented by amount in Row (11) 5.18 %".
  - "A total of approximately $23,061,256 was paid to acquire the Common Shares reported herein." That's about $2.89 a share (arith).
  - "All transactions were effectuated in the open market through a broker", from 4 Sep to 3 Nov at $2.81-2.95 ([Schedule A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1059386/000106299326005733/exhibit2.htm)).
- **What Saba says it may do:**
  - "the Reporting Person may seek to influence the Issuer to engage in share buy-backs, tenders, and liquidations and/or to change its governance, investment strategy and/or investment manager (which may be the Reporting Person)."
  - "The Reporting Persons may make binding or non-binding shareholder proposals, or may nominate one or more individuals as nominees for election to the Board".
- **The dates that matter** ([2026 proxy](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1059386/000119312526271417/d101036ddef14a.htm)):
  - Rule 14a-8 proposals for the 2027 meeting must be received "by March 2, 2027".
  - Other proposals and nominations: "not earlier than April 6, 2027 and not later than May 6, 2027".
- **The discount:** $2.86 on 4 Nov against a net asset value of $3.27 (data: Yahoo's VVR NAV series, XVVRX). The price is about **12.5% below** what the fund owns (arith).
- **Absences:** ~~VVR's EDGAR record shows no earlier activist filing. Its proxies from 2020 to 2026 are board-only meetings, and the 2024 one changed investment restrictions. This is the first push.~~ **CORRECTION (6 Nov):** wrong. Saba ran a campaign at VVR in 2019 (13D at 8.25%, a proposal, four trustee nominees). It ended in a standstill and a tender for 15% of the shares at 98.5% of NAV, with 25.7% of tendered shares accepted. We searched only recent filings under the new form names. See `research/daily/2026-11-06.md` and LESSONS 2026-11-06.
- **Reaction:** +1.4% since the 2 Nov event date vs IWM -2.9%. Two months of Saba buying didn't lift the price (about $2.92 down to $2.81). No news story found.
- **Screen:** $2.85; 20-day median $2.67M a day. Takeable.
- **What would pay:** a tender near asset value, or a liquidation, would close most of the 12.5% gap (inference). Saba names both. **CORRECTION (6 Nov):** the 2019 precedent was a pro-rated 15% tender, which closes only a small part of the gap for one holder. See the 6 Nov note.
- **What could go wrong:**
  - The fund holds leveraged senior loans, so its asset value falls when credit weakens. Days like 5 Nov hit the asset value, not just the discount.
  - Saba could sell, or settle for a small buyback.
  - Nothing may happen before the 2027 meeting.
  - Monthly distributions may be withheld for a UK holder. Whether they count as exempt "interest-related dividends" is not checked yet.
- **Bet before the news (rule 2026-11-04, ACB):**
  - Buying now means owning a loan fund for months, with the discount as the margin of safety.
  - At a ~£50 size the ~3.5% round trip uses up more than a quarter of the gap (arith).
  - The trigger below is Saba's next filing. A proposal or nomination is usually smaller news than a tender (inference), so there should still be time to act on the filing day.
- **Linked names:**
  - VVR -> Saba -> **TCPC** (Saba 5.11% since Oct; TCPC trades at 0.76x NAV after a 14.7% NAV fall this quarter).
  - VVR -> Saba -> **LEO** (Saba about 10.5%, buying at an 8-10% NAV discount).
  - Saba is running several fund campaigns at once.
- **Verdict:** **WATCH, T022.** Trigger: by 6 May 2027, Saba files a 13D/A on VVR that amends Item 4 (a letter, a 14a-8 proposal or a trustee nomination); re-read for OWN on the filing day. No entry now.
