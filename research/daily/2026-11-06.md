# Research: Fri 6 Nov 2026

**Headline:** A correction first. Yesterday we called Saba's 13D on VVR "the first push"; it isn't. Saba ran a full campaign at VVR in 2019, and it ended in a tender for 15% of the shares at 98.5% of NAV, with only 25.7% of tendered shares taken. A repeat would add about 2.8% to a holding, which is less than our trading costs, so VVR drops to **tracked** (T022), and a new rule covers how we check a company's history. ## 1. VVR: Saba has done this before, and the precedent pays little

- **2019, from VVR's own EDGAR history** (the older filings we missed yesterday):
  - Saba filed a 13D in February 2019: "On February 8, 2019, Saba Capital submitted to the Issuer a proposal" ([13D](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1059386/000106299319000562/sc13d.htm)).
  - It gave "notice informing the Issuer of its intention to nominate" four trustees ([13D/A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1059386/000106299319001752/sched13da-vvr.htm)).
  - The standstill in June 2019, with Saba at "12.12%": the fund would "commence a tender offer to purchase for cash 15% of its outstanding Common Shares at a price per Common Share equal to 98.5% of the Issuer's net asset value", and Saba agreed "to withdraw its proposed nominees" ([13D/A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1059386/000090266419002796/p19-1447sc13da.htm)).
  - The result in December 2019: "approximately 105,117,523 Common Shares, or approximately 58.4% ... were tendered", paid at "$4.58 per share", and "Under final pro-ration, 25.7% of the Common Shares tendered will be accepted" ([result](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1059386/000168035919000697/ex99a5iii.htm)).
  - About four months from 13D to standstill, and about ten months to the payout (arith).
- **What a repeat is worth:** if 25.7% of a holding is bought at 98.5% of NAV while the stock sits at an 11% discount, the gain is about 0.257 x 11.07% = **2.8%** on the whole holding (arith). That's below the ~3.5% round trip on a £50 trade. A real payoff needs the discount itself to narrow, and nothing so far says it will.
- **Other facts from the new half-year report** ([N-CSRS, 6 Nov](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1059386/000119312526450894/d172067dncsrs.htm)):
  - "During the six months ended August 31, 2026, the Trust did not sell any common shares through its shelf offering." The 2025 programme allows "up to 77,000,000" shares, but "the Fund may not sell any Common Shares at a price below the current net asset value" ([424B5](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1059386/000110465925026711/tm258538d1_424b5.htm)). It's no supply threat while the fund trades at a discount.
  - The buyback was renewed: "up to 25% of the 20-day average trading volume ... when the Trust is trading at a 10% or greater discount". The share count didn't change in six months, so it wasn't used (arith).
  - For a UK holder: "Non-Resident Alien Shareholders Qualified Interest Income** 80.85 %" ([annual report](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1059386/000119312526213473/d119503dncsr.htm)). Most of the income would come without US withholding, if the broker applies it (not confirmed).
- **Now:** $2.90 against a $3.27 NAV, an 11.3% discount (arith).
- **TCPC, same activist:** Saba's 13D/A of 6 Nov shows "7.14 %", up from "5.11 %", including "11/5/2026 Buy 698,126 4.10" on results day. "This Amendment No. 1 amends Items 3, 5, and 7", so Item 4 is unchanged. TCPC trades at $4.30 against a $5.61 NAV (0.766x, arith) during "its ongoing strategic review" ([13D/A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1370755/000106299326005791/xslSCHEDULE_13D_X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- **Verdict:** **VVR downgraded to tracked**. T022 stays as a re-read: a Saba 13D/A that amends Item 4. TCPC tracked. The X post about VVR stays accurate ("Watching, not a call").

- **Verdict:** **tracked, no call** for both. Neither has a dated trigger that comes before the price would react. FBRT's December dividend and buyback decision is the next re-read.
