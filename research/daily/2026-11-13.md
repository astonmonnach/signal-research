## 3. MYPS: a buyback tender a UK holder shouldn't use
- **The terms:** a modified Dutch auction at "not less than $4.30 per share and not more than $5.50 per share" for up to $15M, which is 3,488,372 shares at the bottom (34.6%) or 2,727,273 at the top (27.1%). It expires "12:00 midnight, New York City time, at the end of the day on December 10, 2026". Holders of fewer than 100 shares get priority. Directors and officers "DO NOT INTEND TO TENDER ANY SHARES". Funded "with cash on hand".
- **The catch:** "the Company intends to treat such payment as a dividend distribution for withholding purposes". At $5.50 a UK holder with a W-8BEN nets $4.675, or $3.85 without it (arith), against $4.99 in the market.
- **Reaction:** $4.29 before (11 Nov), $4.81 on 12 Nov (+12.1%), $4.99 on 13 Nov. $250k a day.
- **Verdict:** tracked, no call. The only angle for a holder is the stock being supported by a buyback of about 30% of the float, which is already partly in the price. Evidence: research/evidence/2026-11-13-MYPS.csv (7 rows, 0 problems).
