## 1. IPW: a floating-price lender, a resale filing for 13.6x the shares, three splits this year

- **What it is.** iPower sells hydroponics and consumer goods online. It is funded by ATW Digital Asset Opportunities XIV, whose Series A notes convert at the lower of the fixed price or "95% of the lowest VWAP during the seven (7) trading day period preceding conversion", with "$15,000,000 of aggregate original principal amount of Series A Convertible Notes remaining available for issuance" ([S-1/A, 30 Oct](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1830072/000168316826008203/ipower_s1a1.htm)). The same filing says $18M elsewhere, which is inconsistent.
- **The supply:**
  - ATW "has converted a total of $11,941,224 of the Series A Convertible Notes, resulting in the conversion of a total of 427,960 shares". That is about 72% of the 596,808 shares outstanding on 30 Oct (arith).
  - The new resale S-1/A covers "Up to 8,089,888 Shares of Common Stock Underlying $3,000,000" of notes, 13.6x the shares (arith). That count assumes a $0.445 floor. At today's price the $3M is nearer 1.8M shares, about 3x the count (arith, inference).
  - A $50M S-3 went effective on 27 Oct. Under the baby-shelf limit it can sell only about $1.70M more in 12 months (arith: one-third of the $10,543,299 float, less $1,815,976 already sold).
  - An S-8 adds 49,372,685 plan shares.
  - ATW is the only seller, capped at 4.99% (9.99% on notice). No leak-out cap was found.
- **Splits:** 1-for-8 (22 May), 1-for-9 (7 Aug) and 1-for-12 (26 Oct) this year, after a 1-for-30 on 27 Oct 2025. Holders have approved splits "up to 1-for-250".
- **What it is not:** the FY2026 10-K has no going-concern doubt ("sufficient liquidity... for at least the next twelve months"), with cash of $478,042 at 30 Jun. The only Nasdaq letter in the past year said it had regained compliance (Nov 2025). The post says neither "going concern" nor "deficiency".
- **Reaction** (Yahoo, split-adjusted):

| 20 Oct | $3.42 | | |
| 27 Oct (S-3 effective) | $2.80 | -18.1% | +1.6% |

- **Public-call screen:**
  - $1.77, over $1.
  - The 20-day median is $430,739 a day (arith), over $150k, but the last two sessions traded only about $130-144k.
  - No 5% holder; insiders 0.45%.

  - All rules pass. The market value is about $1.1M (arith), as small and squeeze-prone as WORX and NIVF; the minimum-size question still waits for AV1.
