## Rules added today

- **Insider clusters over 30 days** (LESSONS, 16 Oct): the scanner's Clusters line now counts different insiders buying the same company within 30 days, using the buy lines of earlier reports as well as the day's own. IMSR was the third miss, after BORR (12 Oct) and FEAM (14 Oct). The 14 and 15 Oct scans were re-run and now show IMSR with 2 and then 3 insiders.
