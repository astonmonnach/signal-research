## 1. NPB: insiders buy below tangible book as the bank exits retail mortgage origination

- **What it is.** Northpointe Bancshares (Grand Rapids; IPO Feb 2025; $545M) is mostly a national mortgage warehouse lender: its Mortgage Purchase Program (MPP) held $4.20B of the $6.79B of loans held for investment at 30 Sep and earned about 69% of Q2 profit (arith) ([Q2 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1336706/000133670626000064/npb-20260630.htm)). Funding is mostly wholesale: brokered deposits are 54.5% of deposits.
- **The cluster** (all code P, no 10b5-1, filed on time 27 Oct):
  - CEO Charles Williams' trust: 100,000 at $15.32-15.42 on 23 and 26 Oct ($1.54M) ([Form 4](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1336706/000133670626000080/0001336706-26-000080-index.htm)). He holds about 9.9% (arith).
  - Director Carrie Boer: 12,000 at $15.40, her first directly held shares ([Form 4](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1336706/000205695426000006/0002056954-26-000006-index.htm)).
  - COO Joseph Long, who joined on 14 Sep: 3,240 at $15.37, his first holding ([Form 4](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1336706/000133670626000081/0001336706-26-000081-index.htm)).
  - The CEO has bought four times before in 2026 ($4.24M at an average $16.91, arith); those buys are about 7% under water. After each one, NPB did +11.8, -0.1, -4.2 and -7.8 points against KRE over 20 days (arith; four cases, not evidence).
- **The same week, from the company:**
  - **Q3 (20 Oct):** EPS $0.39 against $0.60 in Q2, after $9.3M of fair-value marks and a $3.1M deposit fraud; adding those back gives about $0.55 (arith). Tangible book $17.32, up 13.7% in a year (arith) ([release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1336706/000133670626000072/npb-202609x8kxexx991.htm)).
  - **Exit plan (Item 2.05):** the retail and consumer-direct origination channels (121 originators) go, with $6-7M of charges, mostly cash severance, in Q4 ([8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1336706/000133670626000072/npb-20261020.htm)).
  - **Buyer signed 26 Oct:** CMG Mortgage pays 15 bp on originations through the channels to 31 Dec 2028 and takes the leases where it can; no upfront price; completion expected by 30 Nov ([8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1336706/000133670626000083/npb-20261026.htm)). At Q3's pace that fee is at most about $3M a year (arith).
  - **Buyback:** $30M to 22 Oct 2027, 5.5% of the market value and about 7 days of median volume (arith) ([release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1336706/000133670626000075/pressrelease-stockrepurcha.htm)). No obligation to buy.
- **What it could mean.** The expense savings ($50-55M a year) and the 2027 margin guide exist only in a third-party transcript of the 21 Oct call, and two summaries disagree, so they carry no weight here. Annualised lost gain-on-sale revenue is $46M on Q3's rate and $66M on Q2's (arith): the savings roughly cover it on Q3's rate and fall short on Q2's. The CFO said there is "not a ton of additional room" on capital.
- **Reaction** (Yahoo daily closes, checked against Massive; peers are our own choice):
  - Q3 day, 21 Oct: -4.1% against KRE flat. It opened -12.5% at $13.80 and recovered intraday.
  - Found day, 27 Oct: +3.5% (KRE -0.2%, IWM +1.2%). 28 Oct: it opened $16.30 and faded to about $15.77.
  - From the 20 Oct close to 28 Oct: +0.1% against KRE +1.6% and IWM +1.5%. One year: -4.7% against KRE +19.1%.
  - Not run: about 3% above the insiders' prices and below tangible book.
- **Valuation:** 0.91x tangible book, 0.87x book, 7.3x annualised nine-month EPS (arith). Consolidated CET1 about 9.1% (company estimate).
- **TF1:** fails on one leg only: the $15.76 close is below the $16.17 50-day average. Entry over $5, -18.4% from the high, pre-move -0.1%. Takeable: 20-day median $4.31M a day.
- **Absences:** no wire story on the October cluster (insider sites stop at the 1 Sep buy); no analyst action since July; the CMG agreement is not filed (8.01, not 1.01); no supply filing since the IPO; no listed options; no NPB row anywhere in our ledgers before today.
- **Second order:** CMG Mortgage (private) gains a 121-person retail channel for a fee. NPB putting more balance sheet into warehouse lending ($4.7-5.0B guided for end-2027, secondary source) means more price competition for MBIN and CUBI (inference). STC, also mortgage-linked, had its own five-insider cluster the same days.
- **Linked names:** none in the graph beyond today's CMG link; the weak same-industry link is SIC 6022 (196 state banks).
- **Verdict:** **tracked, no call.** **T017 (TRACKED):** by 30 Nov an 8-K reports the CMG sale complete (or the Q3 10-Q, due about 16 Nov, confirms the exit with charges still within $6-7M), and NPB closes above its 50-day average with the other three TF1 legs passing: then re-read for WATCH. At a flat price the 50-day average falls below the price around 18 Nov (arith). Added to watch/watchlist.json and the govfilings list.

## 2. ARTV: a failed cell therapy priced at 69% of its cash, with liquidation on the table

- **What happened.** On 22 Oct the board stopped AlloNK, including the planned Phase 3 ([8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1817241/000119312526432806/d102135d8k.htm), [release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1817241/000119312526432806/d102135dex991.htm)).
  - Two patients died (Grade 5). One case was likely fludarabine toxicity, after which the FDA asked for a pause in the Phase 2a. It was also about efficacy: longer follow-up "did not reflect the level of durability" needed.
  - About 94% of staff go. Leerink is advising on the options: a merger, a business combination, a sale or licence of assets, or dissolution and liquidation. No deadline, and no updates until the board picks a course.
- **The hard number.** Preliminary cash and investments of $319.1M at 30 Sep, on 51,060,259 shares and pre-funded warrants, is **$6.25 a share against $4.30**: the price is 69% of gross cash (arith). There is no debt and no CVR.
- **What it costs to get the cash out.** The company says it "cannot reasonably estimate" the wind-down costs (contract and trial close-out, leases, severance), most of which fall in Q4. It must file an 8-K/A within four business days of having an estimate. From the filed numbers (Q2 10-Q, 10-K, proxy) and labelled assumptions:

  | | Generous | Mid | Harsh |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Cash and investments, 30 Sep (8-K) | 319.1 | 319.1 | 319.1 |
  | Payables and accruals (30 Jun) | -11.3 | -11.3 | -11.3 |
  | Lease ($10.2M of minimum payments left; 50%, 75%, full) | -5.1 | -7.7 | -10.2 |
  | Q4 run-off (Q3 burn $30.3M), inference | -10 | -15 | -20 |
  | Severance (about 98 staff plus executive terms), inference | -5 | -8 | -13 |

  The price implies about $99.5M (31%) of leakage, more than the harsh case, so it already carries some risk that the cash is spent or merged away (arith; inference).
- **Who decides.** RA Capital holds 37.9% plus pre-funded warrants (about 40% economic) and a board seat; it bought $100.0M of the May offering at $11.52 and more in the market at $6.77-10.00, about $118M in all against about $88M today (arith) ([13D/A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1817241/000134682426000203/xslSCHEDULE_13D_X02/primary_doc.xml)). GC Corp holds 12.3%. Specialist funds and GC hold about 66%; free float about 34% (arith). A plan of dissolution or a reverse merger needs a vote, where RA is close to decisive (inference).
- **Precedents, not a rate** (no study of outcome frequencies found):
  - Liquidation: THRD (2025) estimated distributions at 91-96% of its cash (arith) ([8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1923840/000119312525079594/d891311dex991.htm)).
  - Cash-shell buyer: Concentra (Tang Capital) paid 94% of minimum net cash plus a CVR for CARGO ([offer](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1966494/000199937125009491/ex99-a1a.htm)).
  - Reverse merger: Acelyrin traded at about 56% of net cash, then merged into Alumis for stock ([8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1962918/000119312525123707/d13977d8k.htm)).
- **Reaction** (Yahoo closes): -30.7% on 27 Oct against XBI +2.9% and IWM +1.2%; volume about 12x the $2.98M median. It had already slid 45% from 4 Sep to 26 Oct, but its named competitors fell 17-61% over the same weeks, so that is weak evidence of a leak (inference). On the day NKTX fell 7.1%, while CABA and KYTX rose 7-8%.
- **Second order:** the company says it will publish the fludarabine case "given its widespread use" in cell-therapy conditioning, a read-across for CAR-T and NK names using fludarabine and cyclophosphamide (inference). GC Cell loses its licensee outside Asia.
- **UK tax** (from THRD's proxy as the Delaware template): a liquidating distribution is generally treated as a sale for a non-US holder, so no US tax on the gain; it would only be a dividend, with withholding, to the extent of earnings and profits, which a company with a $379.1M deficit very likely lacks (inference). After dissolution the shares stop trading.
- **Absences:** no cost estimate, no timetable, no promise to return cash, no buyback; no activist 13D and no RA 13D/A since the news; no cash-shell tender; no insider buying (the last Form 4s were 5-6 Oct sales); no wire story on the gap.
- **Options:** listed (first expiry 20 Nov). Thin 27 Oct prints imply about 100-157% volatility (arith). **View: smaller** than implied around the 10-Q, which mostly confirms cash on a $4.46-5.48 net range; the big move comes from a board decision the company says it won't pre-announce (inference). There is no row for it in ledger/implied_moves.csv, so the view is recorded here only.
- **Linked names:** ALLO, CABA, FATE, IBRX, KYTX, NKTX, SANA (named competitors in the 10-K) and LYCA (ARTV -> Venrock, a 13G holder in both -> LYCA). Logged as linked_peer.
- **TF1:** fails three legs (entry $4.35, 68% below the high, below the 50-day). Takeable on liquidity.
- **Verdict:** **tracked, no call.** **T018 (TRACKED):** the Q3 10-Q (due 16 Nov) or an 8-K/A gives a wind-down estimate and net cash works out at $5.00 a share or more: re-read for WATCH. Re-read at once on an activist 13D, an RA 13D/A changing its purpose, a cash-shell tender, a plan of dissolution or a merger agreement. Added to watch/watchlist.json and the govfilings list.
