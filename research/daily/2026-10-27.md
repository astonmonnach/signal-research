## 1. The AVOID record, cleaned and tested

The trader asked whether AVOIDs that later have +10% days mean the calibration is wrong, and whether some are "just could go either way".

- **The register:** `ledger/avoid_register.csv`, every AVOID ever given, compiled from every note and triage file. 77 on 27 Oct, 89 with today's.
  - Only 14 had ever been scored.
  - 47 active AVOIDs were missing from IBKR's AVOID list, which still held a reversed one (TKNO) and two killed long theses (CHDN, THRM).
  - The list now mirrors the register, logged in `watch/IBKR_LISTS_LOG.md`.
- **Since flagged, against IWM** (72 priced; many only 1-5 days old):

  | type | behind IWM | median vs IWM |
  |---|---|---|
  | distress | 11/12 | -24.4 pts |
  | deal dilution | 4/4 | -10.6 |
  | ongoing supply | 25/30 | -4.6 |
  | holder selling | 2/4 | -2.3 |
  | one-off offering | 5/12 | **+2.9** |

- **Against matched unflagged stocks** (AV1, frozen in [PREREG_AV1.md](../../ledger/PREREG_AV1.md) before any comparison; description only, no row judged yet). Each AVOID is compared with the median of up to 10 stocks in the same price bucket at half to twice its market value. Full table in [AV1_PROGRESS.md](../../ledger/AV1_PROGRESS.md).
  - Distress: median -11.1 pts, 8/10 behind.
  - Ongoing supply: -3.2, 19/30 behind.
  - One-off offerings: **+5.6**, 5/13 behind.
  - So part of the raw record is "tiny stocks fall anyway", but distress and ongoing supply still beat their lookalikes.
- **Big up days are normal on the way down:** +10% days came on about 3% of sessions. GWH had a +25.7% day and is -59 pts.
- **Misses:**
  - AIOK, +216%. An AVOID on a 74% insider's resale registration; the insider declared a $3.50 special dividend instead and the small float squeezed.
  - CHDN and THRM: killed long theses, not supply.
  - TKNO, a one-off offering, was reversed on 22 Oct.
- **Verdict:** AVOID calls continue, narrowed by two rules (RULES.md 2026-10-27):
  - public calls only for ongoing supply, warrant supply or distress;
  - never on a one-off offering that is already priced;
  - never on an insider-controlled or tiny-float stock.

  A killed long thesis is "dropped, no call", not an AVOID.
