## Rules added today

- **A 13D from merger shares, or from the buyer in a deal signed weeks earlier, is not activism.** BBCI (de-SPAC closing shares) and two PUSA filers (merger shares) sit in the scanner's Activists section. So does ITGR, where KKR filed 10 weeks after signing its $127 cash deal because its swaps settled late. The ledger's 6 Oct rule only drops a 13D filed within 3 days of a signed merger, so all four would count as buyable activist signals. LESSONS.md row added. Not in code: it changes the ledger's scoring, so the trader decides.
- FEAM is the second case of the 12 Oct cluster miss (two insiders, one day, filed on different days). That rule is still open in code.
