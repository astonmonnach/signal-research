## 1. GRRR: floating-price notes four months after $25 convertibles

- **The deal** (6-K, 30 Oct):
  - Up to $111.1M of notes "for an aggregate funding amount of up to $100,000,000".
  - They convert at "90% of the average of the three lowest daily volume-weighted average prices of the Ordinary Shares during the 20 trading days prior to conversion, subject to a cap of $6,000,000 in conversions per month", with a floor of $3.858.
  - The first note, $27.775M, is issued. With it came 1,000,000 "Pre-Delivery Shares for an aggregate purchase price of $1,000". Up to $83.3M more by mutual agreement.
  - On default, the conversion price drops to 80%.
  - The company must lift authorised shares "from 73,500,000 to 200,000,000".
- **Why it matters:** in June it sold convertibles at "approximately $25.4826" ([6-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1903145/000121390026079193/ea0298371-6k_gorilla.htm)). It now takes money priced off its lowest trades. At the floor, the full programme is about 1.0-1.1x the 26,188,972 shares it had at end-2025 (arith; that is the last stated count).
- **Screen:**
  - $7.54 on 30 Oct; 20-day median $10.8M a day.
  - The last annual report lists "Five Percent or More Holders: N/A".
  - Ongoing supply, not a one-off. Eligible.
- **Reaction:** 28 Oct $10.08, 29 Oct $9.14, 30 Oct $7.54 (-17.5% on the day vs IWM -1.6%; -25.2% over three days).
- **Verdict:** **AVOID, public call #11.**
  - Invalidation: a close above **$10.08** (28 Oct, the last close before the notes).
  - Control: IGV. Review 30 Nov.
  - Dated checks: resale registration due about 29 Nov; VWAP conversions open by about 29 Dec; the authorised-share meeting is due by about 13 Jan (arith, 30/60/75 days from closing).

## 3. CABO: a restructuring officer joins the strategic-alternatives committee

- **The 8-K** (filed 16:35 ET on 30 Oct, after the close, so Monday is the first reaction): the board named Mr. Keglevic "to serve as a member of a special committee of the Board established to evaluate strategic alternatives". He "previously served as its Chief Restructuring Officer" (at EFH) and gets a "monthly cash fee of $40,000" ([8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1632127/000095015726001140/form8-k.htm)).
- **The build-up:**
  - In September it "borrowed $700.0 million under the $1.25 billion revolving credit facility... to increase cash on hand and preserve financial flexibility".
  - On 2 Oct, "engagement with those potential investors has concluded", and the MBI purchase closing was moved to 9 Oct. We found no 8-K confirming that closing.
  - The 10-Q lists options "including raising additional funds, amending existing debt agreements". $1.59B matures in 2028.
- **What we did not find:** going-concern language, a covenant breach (it was "in compliance with all debt covenants as of June 30"), forbearance, missed payments or named restructuring advisers. The 15 Nov coupon and the Q3 10-Q (due about 9 Nov) are the next checks.
- **Screen:**
  - $5.46; 20-day median $5.42M a day.
  - Largest holder D.E. Shaw at 8.4%; the Graham trusts at 4.99%; insiders 0.9%.
  - Eligible. CABO was already on the register (20 Oct, holder selling); this is its first public call.
- **Reaction:** -55.3% over 20 days vs IWM -0.8%, but +13.0% over the last 5. It trades like an option, and "strategic alternatives" could still mean a sale that leaves the equity something.
- **Verdict:** **AVOID, public call #13.**
  - Invalidation: a close above **$7.25** (above the 16 Oct close and the 20-22 Oct highs).
  - Control: CHTR. Review 30 Nov.

## 5. LEE: a 13D with no new shares

- **What the filing shows:** Quint Digital "beneficially owned 3,244,800 Common Stock, representing approximately 14.38%". It reports "(c) No such transactions" in 60 days, and it "has adopted a Rule 10b5-1 trading plan" (29 Oct). It mentions board talks, but "does not currently intend to seek control".
- **What it means:** Quint bought its main block in the Feb 2026 PIPE at $3.25. Its standstill runs to about 5 Feb 2027, with a 600,000-share market allowance (about 23 days of trading, arith). Hoffmann controls 53.47%.
- **The company:** debt is one $454.7M Berkshire-affiliate loan to 2045, with "no leverage or financial performance covenants"; cash $59.4M.
- **Reaction:** none (-1.6% vs IWM -1.65%). Liquidity is marginal ($165k a day).
- **Verdict:** **tracked, no call.** Re-read if a Quint Form 4 shows buying above 3,244,800 shares before 5 Feb 2027. Plan purchases can't start before about 28 Nov (inference: cooling-off).
