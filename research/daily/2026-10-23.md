## 2. CPAC: Peru's regulator queries the valuation behind the minimum price

- **The filing:** [6-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1221029/000121390026111473/ea030936201ex99-1.htm) with SMV Official Letter 5332-2026, dated 21 Oct.
- **What the SMV asks** the valuer, MSRA:
  - to explain why it used the Moore Hong Kong premium rather than BVR's;
  - to justify its cash-flow assumptions: volume growth tied to private investment, 0.5% price growth, a severe El Niño every 5 years, 3.9% capital spending from 2033, and a cost of debt taken from bonds only;
  - to justify its land and liquidation adjustments.
- MSRA had one business day to answer. The letter warns of liability for errors "that result in a modification of the minimum price".
- **How the price was set** (Offer to Purchase): the valuation's cash-flow method gave S/8.63 a share and its multiples method S/7.01. S/8.93, the price Holcim paid in the deal that triggered the offer, is the floor.
- **Both ways (inference):**
  - If the cash-flow value is revised up by more than 3.5% (above S/8.93), the legal minimum rises above the offer.
  - If the SMV suspends or invalidates the Peru offer, that breaks a condition of the US offer and delays payment.
- **Price:** $12.77, +0.9%. At about 3.432 PEN/USD the offer is worth about $13.01, a 1.9% gross spread (arith).
- **Verdict:** stays **WATCH** (T011). The board's report is due about 2 Nov (arith), and MSRA's reply is the next check.
