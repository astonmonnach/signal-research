## 3. What it shows

- **The day after is a coin flip with a wide range.** Across 193 rows the median move from the next open to the next close is +0.0%; 68% touched +5% above the open and 70% touched -5%.
- **Then most of them give it back.** Median -10.5% after 5 sessions and -22.7% after 10, from the gain-day close. Takeable rows: -1.9% and -13.0%.
- **No-news gainers fall hardest.** 104 rows: median -16.3% after 5 sessions and -26.2% after 10 (11 of 59 up). Most cannot be shorted from a small UK account, so this is a don't-chase result.
- **Earnings gainers are the one group that kept going the next day.** 24 rows: next close +4.5% against the gain-day close (17 of 24 up); takeable ones +4.2% (13 of 18 up), and +2.0% from the next open (11 of 18 up). Only 10 have 5 later sessions (median -2.5%), and most came from one results week (2 to 9 Nov). A lead to keep logging, not a rule.
- **Signed deals stop moving.** 10 rows: next-day range +0.6% up and -0.3% down from the open. Those are merger-arbitrage spreads, not runners.
- **No verdicts or deep dives follow from this.** The next read is at 40 sessions; the earnings group is the one to check then.
