## 1. Who is selling?
Corteva holders who got 1 VYLR share per CTVA share on 1 Oct 2026. About 667M shares land at once in accounts that never asked for them.

## 2. Why are they forced, not choosing?
- Index funds: the filing says they "may be required to sell" if VYLR isn't in their index. No S&P decision has been found yet.
- Crop-protection / dividend-focused CTVA holders who don't want a seed business.
- No when-issued market, so there was no price discovery before day one.
